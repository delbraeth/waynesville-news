## This morning in Waynesville

1. **School board buys a bus, ends Cintas contracts.** At its October 5 meeting, the Wayne Local Board of Education declared an urgent need for an additional school bus and approved buying one for $145,525, paid for with unspent capital-project borrowing that came in under budget after planned vans were unavailable. The board also approved a $27,818.04 settlement ending the district's contracts with Cintas; the district says buying its own rugs, towels and bathroom dispensers instead of renting them should save about $40,000 to $70,000 a year. Source: Wayne Local Board Meeting Recap (Oct. 8, 2026). *([wayne-local.com](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
2. **Pink Out tonight.** Waynesville hosts Madison at 7:00 p.m. at Spartan Community Field in the WHS Pink Out game for breast cancer awareness. There is no school for students today (teacher professional day) or Monday (fall break). *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/madison-middletown-vs-waynesville/10-9-2026/?c=077b01be-2f75-46e9-8dba-5dd51aa90d48))*
3. **Sauerkraut Festival opens Saturday.** The 56th Ohio Sauerkraut Festival runs downtown Saturday from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. and Sunday from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., per the Chamber. The National Weather Service calls for mostly cloudy skies and a chance of showers Saturday (high 77°) and showers and thunderstorms Sunday (high 73°). *([Chamber](https://www.waynesvilleohio.com/events))*

## Schools
The Board Meeting Recap from the October 5 meeting leads with the district's 2025–26 Ohio School Report Card: Superintendent Dr. Ison reported an overall rating of 4.5 stars, with Waynesville High School and Waynesville Elementary holding 5 stars and Waynesville Middle School rising from 3.5 to 4. He said the Progress component, which measures whether students show the academic growth the state expects and counts for 25% of the overall rating, will be a key focus this year. The board approved revisions to the WMS/WHS student handbook that set expectations for student use of artificial intelligence and AI-enabled devices such as smart glasses, along with consequences for unauthorized use. Also approved: a $5,700 donation from the Waynesville PTO for art, music, PE, computer and PBIS activities; a five-month facility dog internship at Waynesville Elementary with Circle Tail, Inc. starting January 1; and overnight trips to Williamsburg, Virginia, for gifted/enrichment students in grades 4–7 and to Washington, D.C., for eighth graders next May. Dr. Ison recognized seven WHS student artists for a large mural in the new movement rooms at Waynesville Elementary, and said turf and track installers need to repair a few areas before the project is finalized. Board members discussed how to handle finance topics, including whether to form a board finance committee, and Dr. Ison said he is arranging a meeting with Warren County Auditor Matt Nolan to share levy information with the board. The board next meets Monday, November 9, at 6:00 p.m. at the WLS Performing Arts Center, opening with the Spartan Recognition Awards. Source: Wayne Local Board Meeting Recap (Oct. 8, 2026).
