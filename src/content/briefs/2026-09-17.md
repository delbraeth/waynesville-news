## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Six Spartan contests are on the schedule tonight** across three sports. Volleyball hosts Valley View (JV 5:30 p.m., varsity 6:30 p.m.) after beating Madison 3–1 at home Tuesday; the soccer teams split the trip, with the boys at Brookville and the girls hosting Brookville, both at 7:15 p.m. ([schedule](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/waynesville/waynesville-spartans/))
2. **The Mary L. Cook Public Library runs two programs this afternoon** — the Early Readers Kindergarten Book Club at 4:00 p.m., and an Armchair Ghost Walk with the Friends Museum at 6:00 p.m. ([library calendar](https://marylcook-main-oh.whofi.com/calendar/))
3. **Today is the last hot one for a while.** The National Weather Service has Waynesville at 90° and mostly sunny with a slight chance of afternoon storms, dropping to a high of 82° Friday — just ahead of a weekend that brings Fall Fest's opening day, the Ren Fest's Pirates Weekend, and the Clean Sweep river cleanup.
