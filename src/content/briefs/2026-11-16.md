## This morning in Waynesville


1. **Region 20 final at Lakota East.** Waynesville, fresh off a 14-7 win at top-seeded Graham Local, meets Bishop Fenwick in the Division V, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. Friday at a neutral site, Lakota East High School in Liberty Township. Tickets are sold online through the OHSAA; check its ticket page for when they go on sale. High school students in grades 9–12 can ride a spirit bus to the game; permission slips from the high school office are due Wednesday. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/bishop-fenwick-franklin-vs-waynesville/11-20-2026/?c=2f2a6d51-5706-4af5-a1bd-43c71e2f70d3) · [OHSAA tickets](https://www.ohsaa.org/tickets) · [spirit bus post](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid0u8NUYiGFNXrAsHrSSDT6Ycr1zUCExa6jM8mNeE1TkZXrpsbB92KVeCbMRM8gL6i3l))*
2. **Last chance on the North Warren County Transportation Plan.** The online survey stays open through Tuesday, Nov. 17. It asks about options for a new I-75 to I-71 connection, one of which would follow US 42 and S.R. 73 through Waynesville and Corwin, and about priority intersections such as S.R. 73 and US 42 in Waynesville. *([survey and plan](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/pages/f27bc61005b842439ceb569b82c0b9cf))*
3. **Council votes on a downtown retaining wall.** Village Council meets at 7 p.m. tonight at the Municipal Building, 1400 Lytle Rd. Up for second reading: an ordinance to split the cost of a retaining wall with the owner of 121 S. Main St. (the Hammel House), with the village paying 50%, up to $30,000. *([agenda packet](https://www.villageofwaynesville.org/media/site/meetings/Council%20Agenda%2011.16.2026.pdf))*
