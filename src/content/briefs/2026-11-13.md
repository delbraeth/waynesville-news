## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Final: Waynesville 14, Graham 7 — on to the regional final.** The Spartans won Friday night's Division V, Region 20 semifinal at Graham High School in St. Paris, after beating Preble Shawnee 35-13 in Round 2, Waynesville Spartans Football reports. In the regional final they meet Bishop Fenwick of Franklin, which won its semifinal 27-13 at Williamsburg; kickoff is 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 20, at a neutral site OHSAA has not yet announced. *([regional final on MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/bishop-fenwick-franklin-vs-waynesville/11-20-2026/?c=2f2a6d51-5706-4af5-a1bd-43c71e2f70d3))* *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d))*
2. **Line Dayton and Lytle roads at 4 p.m.** Waynesville Football invites the whole village to wear orange and black for the All-Village Spartan Send Off as the team buses leave Waynesville High School at 4 p.m., turn left out of the school and head out of town on Lytle Road. The forecast calls for a chance of light rain with a high of 46 and a low of 29. *([send-off flyer](/images/events/2026-11-13-spartan-send-off.webp))*
3. **Fantasy Festival and Owl Fest Saturday.** The free Waynesville Fantasy Festival fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Caesar Creek State Park's Owl Fest runs from 3 to 11 p.m. Saturday's forecast is sunny with a high of 47. *([festival map](/images/events/2026-11-14-fantasy-festival-map.png) · [Owl Fest](https://ohiodnr.gov/home/news-and-events/all-events/parks-wc-events/SW-Ohio/OwlFestCaesarCreek-11-14-26.))*
