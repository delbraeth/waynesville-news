## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Election Day is Tuesday.** Polls are open 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. on Tuesday, November 3; early voting has ended. Waynesville-area ballots carry renewal levies for the Mental Health Recovery Board (1 mill, five years) and Warren County Senior Services (1.21 mills, five years), plus a statewide constitutional amendment on photo ID for voting. *([Board of Elections notice](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
2. **Village Council meets tonight.** Waynesville Village Council meets at 7:00 p.m. at the Waynesville Municipal Building, 1400 Lytle Road. The posted agenda lists approval of the October 19 minutes, visitor comments, and committee, village manager, police, finance and law director reports. *([agenda](https://www.villageofwaynesville.org/media/site/meetings/Council%20Agenda%2011.02.2026.pdf))*
3. **Holiday season starts downtown Friday.** Hearthwarming Holidays runs Friday through Sunday, November 6–8, in historic downtown Waynesville; check the event page for each day's hours. *([Merchants Association](https://waynesvilleshops.com/event/hearthwarming-holidays-in-waynesville-ohio/))*
