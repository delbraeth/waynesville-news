## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Spartans win the region, 27-0.** Waynesville shut out Bishop Fenwick on Friday night at Lakota East to win the Division V, Region 20 title, and the athletic department says the Spartans head to the state semifinals for the first time in school history. State Final Four shirts can be ordered through 9 p.m. Sunday for pickup Wednesday, 3 to 5 p.m.; watch Spartans Athletics for the semifinal matchup and site. *([box score](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/bishop-fenwick-franklin-vs-waynesville/11-20-2026/?c=2f2a6d51-5706-4af5-a1bd-43c71e2f70d3) · [Spartans Athletics](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid0k79Y9U2NnxGTYfsbPnX3fJbe8mA6LeHp7sgoqJBUawDSfFxA8yzwziCF8eiaGosBl))*
2. **Wayne Local sets a levy listening session.** The district's November newsletter says its forecast for this school year projects about $20.4 million in revenue and $22.1 million in expenses, drawing about $1.7 million from reserves, which are projected to be nearly exhausted by the end of fiscal 2030. A Community Levy Listening Session is set for 6 p.m. Monday, Dec. 7, in the Performing Arts Center lobby, to gather input on May or November 2027 timing and a property tax versus an earned income tax; the board takes the question up again Dec. 14. *(The Spartan, Wayne Local Schools newsletter (November 2026) · [Wayne Local Schools](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
3. **Stars Hollow weekend continues.** Town Square Days runs 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. today and 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday downtown, under sunny skies and a forecast high near 68. The Town Square Days Dance Marathon at Turn Out Dance Academy on South Main Street starts at 11:30 a.m. today. *([event map](/images/events/2026-11-21-town-square-days-map.webp))*

## Schools

The newsletter also says Warren County Career Center applications for 2027–28 open Dec. 2, and that bus riders' families should keep My Ride K12 app notifications on, since urgent transportation notices go out only through the app. *([Wayne Local Schools](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
