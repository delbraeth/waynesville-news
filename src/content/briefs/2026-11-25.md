## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Weigh in on the next school levy.** Wayne Local Schools will hold a Community Levy Listening Session on Monday, Dec. 7, from 6 to 7:30 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center lobby, the district said in its Nov. 24 Weekly Update. The district will share its financial outlook and ask for input on two questions for 2027: whether to go to voters in May or November, and whether to seek a property tax or an earned income tax. Board President Amanda Garrett will lead the discussion with Treasurer Carolyn Huber and Superintendent Sam Ison, and the feedback will inform the board's Dec. 14 meeting. *([Wayne Local Schools](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
2. **Troopers out in force for the holiday.** The Ohio State Highway Patrol is focusing on impaired and distracted drivers from midnight tonight through 11:59 p.m. Sunday, Nov. 29, the Warren County Post reports. The National Weather Service forecasts light rain on Thanksgiving Day with a high near 47 and more light rain Friday. *([Warren County Post](https://warrencountypost.com/g/lebanon-oh/n/399207/troopers-focus-impaired-distracted-driving-over-thanksgiving-holiday) · [National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891))*
3. **Read up before Friday's trip.** Wayne Local and Spartans Athletics posted game-day information for fans heading to Friday's Division V state semifinal against Ironton at 7 p.m. at Teays Valley High School, and ask everyone attending to read it first. The All-Village Send-Off starts when the buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 p.m. *([Wayne Local Schools](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid02jXwz3aF8Vj8G1WQFBfg3azuHiApsm1xiKqfSkRfiSzfQBzUgeidngcDrKMkq6cSzl) · [Spartans Athletics](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid0CzTD3Kb4qvEQMRpVKLWLgyDw7rqjXSAtHA4HphMKJ5yQE9vr2S8bCjDRWGDG3NT9l))*
