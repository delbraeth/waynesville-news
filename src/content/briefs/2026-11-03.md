## This morning in Waynesville

1. **It's Election Day.** Polls are open until 7:30 p.m. Waynesville-area ballots carry renewal levies for the Mental Health Recovery Board (1 mill, five years) and Warren County Senior Services (1.21 mills, five years), plus a statewide constitutional amendment on photo ID for voting. Unofficial results will post on the county results page after polls close. *([Board of Elections notice](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf) · [results](https://liveresults.boe.ohio.gov/ENR/warrenohenr/18/en/R_Index_18.html))*
   *Update: both county levies and the statewide photo-ID amendment passed, according to unofficial results — details under Local government below.*
2. **Wayne Township Trustees meet tonight.** The trustees meet at 7:00 p.m. at 6050 N. Clarksville Rd. The agenda outline the township posted opens with approval of the October 20 minutes and up to 30 minutes of citizen comments. *([township post](https://www.facebook.com/waynetownshipus/posts/pfbid034W7ro733LpNywgHUgTumbsGC6ArnwueemSk7FxKcpQ9xk2mZCVmfyRMn8Piyeh4Yl))*

**Update, Wednesday morning — unofficial results:** Both county renewal levies passed. The Senior Services levy (Issue 7) drew 74,247 yes votes to 35,358 no (67.7%), and the Mental Health Recovery Board levy (Issue 6) drew 67,917 to 38,403 (63.9%), with all precincts reporting, the [Warren County Post reports](https://warrencountypost.com/g/lebanon-oh/n/397174/warren-county-voters-renew-mental-health-and-senior-services-levies). Statewide, the photo-ID amendment (Issue 3) passed by a 2-1 margin, [Statehouse News Bureau reports](https://www.statenews.org/government-politics/2026-11-04/amendment-requiring-photo-id-for-in-person-voting-in-ohio-passes-easily). Results stay unofficial until the Board of Elections certifies them ([official results](https://liveresults.boe.ohio.gov/ENR/warrenohenr/18/en/R_Index_18.html)).
