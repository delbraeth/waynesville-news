## This morning in Waynesville

1. **School board meets tonight.** The Wayne Local Board of Education holds its regular monthly meeting at 6:00 p.m. in the auditorium of the WLS Performing Arts Center, 625 Dayton Rd., with Spartan Recognition Awards on the program. *([district](https://www.wayne-local.com/) · [BoardDocs](https://go.boarddocs.com/oh/waylo/Board.nsf/Public))*
2. **Roads plan comes to the village Tuesday.** Warren County Engineer Kurt Weber will discuss the North Warren County Transportation Plan Tuesday at 7:00 p.m. at the Village of Waynesville office, 1400 Lytle Rd., according to the Chamber, which asks attendees to RSVP. Local 12 reports planners have drawn six proposals for a better I-71/I-75 connection across the northern county, including one that would follow S.R. 122 east to U.S. 42 and then S.R. 73, and that any construction is years away. The plan's online survey closes November 17. *([Chamber](https://www.waynesvilleohio.com/events) · [Local 12](https://local12.com/news/local/warren-county-seeks-input-on-proposals-improve-i-71-i-75-connection-regional-transportation-plan-construction-building-cost-price-traffic-detour-cars-vehicles-semi-trucks-lebanon-cities-roads-advantages-issues-wilmington-road-cincinnati) · [survey](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/pages/f27bc61005b842439ceb569b82c0b9cf))*
3. **Semifinal Friday, festival Saturday.** Fresh off a 35-13 playoff win over Preble Shawnee, Spartan football plays a regional semifinal at Graham Local in St. Paris Friday at 7:00 p.m. On Saturday the Waynesville Fantasy Festival fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. *([next game](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d) · [events calendar](/events/))*
