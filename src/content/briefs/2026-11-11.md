## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Wayne Local salutes veterans this morning.** The district has invited veterans and active-duty military members to a Veterans Day breakfast from 7:45 to 8:30 a.m., a program from 8:50 to 9:30 a.m. and a veterans car parade from 9:30 to 10:00 a.m., according to its Facebook page. Wayne Township's administrative offices are closed today for the holiday. *([district post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid02PFJzSEid65LiQnTUUn97uZ57X3hJccWm59CPnw2fKEduqBjQb4PhGpBC4Rktm2Anl) · [township notice](https://waynetwpwarrencooh.gov/veterans-day-observation/))*
2. **Spartans chase a spot in the regional final Friday.** Waynesville (9-2) travels to Graham High School in St. Paris for a Division V, Region 20 semifinal Friday at 7:00 p.m., after beating Preble Shawnee 35-13. Before the trip, the community is invited to line Lytle Road in orange and black for an All-Village Spartan Send Off; the buses leave the high school at 4 p.m. The forecast calls for a low of 28 Friday night; OHSAA sells playoff tickets online. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d) · [send-off flyer](/images/events/2026-11-13-spartan-send-off.webp))*
3. **A full Saturday close to home.** The free Waynesville Fantasy Festival runs downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., Caesar Creek State Park hosts Owl Fest from 3 to 11 p.m. starting at the Visitor Center, and CrossView Christian Church holds a dessert auction from 6 to 8 p.m. for the Miami Cemetery Association Roads Fund. *([events calendar](/events/) · [Owl Fest](https://ohiodnr.gov/home/news-and-events/all-events/parks-wc-events/SW-Ohio/OwlFestCaesarCreek-11-14-26.))*
