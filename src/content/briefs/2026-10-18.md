## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Freeze warning until 9 a.m., then a sunny, warming week.** The National Weather Service in Wilmington has a Freeze Warning in effect until 9:00 a.m. today, with widespread frost this morning. After that, the forecast turns sunny with a high near 64° today, and highs of 68° Monday, 69° Tuesday and 70° Wednesday, though more frost is possible early Monday. Village leaf pickup begins Monday; rake leaves to the curb, not into the street. *([NWS](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891) · [Village of Waynesville](https://www.villageofwaynesville.org/news/entry/leaf-pick-up/))*
2. **Last day of Romance Weekend, and fossils at Caesar Creek.** The Ohio Renaissance Festival's Romance Weekend wraps up today, 10:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.; Myths & Monsters Weekend follows October 24–25. At Caesar Creek, the ODNR Geological Survey leads guided fossil collecting for Earth Science Week at the Lake Visitor Center from 1:00 to 4:00 p.m., and the state park has Campfire and Craft at 10:00 a.m. and Rocks and Relics at 1:00 p.m. *([Renaissance Festival](https://www.renfestival.com/festival-info/themed-weekends/) · [Campfire and Craft](https://ohiodnr.gov/home/news-and-events/all-events/parks-wc-events/SW-Ohio/CampfireandCraftCaesarCreek-10-18-26.) · [Rocks and Relics](https://ohiodnr.gov/home/news-and-events/all-events/parks-wc-events/SW-Ohio/RocksandRelicsCaesarCreek-10-18-26))*
3. **A busy civic Monday and Tuesday.** Village Council meets Monday at 7:00 p.m.; the draft minutes in its agenda packet record that council adopted Ordinance 2026-043 as an emergency at its previous meeting, 7-0, authorizing the village manager and finance director to open a village credit card account with Peoples Bank and enroll in the bank's Insured Cash Sweep service. Early in-person voting resumes Monday, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., at the Board of Elections in Lebanon, and the county commissioners hold a 2027 budget work session Tuesday. *([council agenda packet](https://www.villageofwaynesville.org/media/site/meetings/Council%20Agenda%2010.19.2026.pdf) · [Board of Elections schedule](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
