## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Book fair family night at Waynesville Elementary.** The school's book fair hosts families from 4 to 7 p.m. today before it wraps up Friday. At 6 p.m., the library's Christmas Book Flood PJ Storytime sends children home with a book. *([school graphic](/images/events/2026-11-wes-upcoming-events.png) · [library](https://www.mlcook.lib.oh.us/))*
2. **Line Lytle Road for the Spartans Friday.** Waynesville Football asks the village to wear orange and black and line Lytle Road as the team buses leave the high school at 4 p.m. for the Division V, Region 20 semifinal at Graham in St. Paris. Kickoff is 7 p.m.; Friday's forecast low is 28. *([Spartans Athletics post](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid02ayoMZXkXSFcTh1HEhLtDHyBwn4ngnnEAbC8DiDQoj83Cr1AdDuWjPJ98ohw2TKKEl) · [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d))*
3. **Fantasy Festival and Owl Fest Saturday.** The free Waynesville Fantasy Festival runs downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Caesar Creek State Park's Owl Fest runs from 3 to 11 p.m., starting at the Visitor Center; the park says the Ohio School of Falconry will be there this weekend starting at 6 p.m. Saturday's forecast is mostly sunny with a high of 46. *([festival map](/images/events/2026-11-14-fantasy-festival-map.png) · [Owl Fest](https://ohiodnr.gov/home/news-and-events/all-events/parks-wc-events/SW-Ohio/OwlFestCaesarCreek-11-14-26.))*
