## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Early release and Senior Night Friday.** Wayne Local students have a two-hour early release Friday to close the first quarter, the district reminded families this week. That night the Spartans, 45-7 winners over Madison last week, host Eaton at 7:00 p.m. on Football Senior Night, honoring football, cheer, band, cross country and boys golf seniors. *([Wayne Local Schools](https://www.wayne-local.com/) · [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/eaton-vs-waynesville/10-16-2026/?c=ae4a8cf1-eaf5-4c11-8eeb-35412dd55d0b))*
2. **First frost of the season likely Sunday.** After a 75° Thursday with a chance of showers, rain showers are likely Friday (high 64°). Saturday turns sunny but cold, with a high of 54° and a low near 33° Saturday night, and the forecast calls for widespread frost Sunday morning before a sunny, 61° afternoon. Bring in tender plants and cover what you can't move. No NWS alerts are in effect this morning. *([NWS](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891))*
3. **Township trustees meet today on zoning hire.** The Wayne Township Board of Trustees' special meeting is today, Thursday, October 15, at the township administration building, 6050 N. Clarksville Rd. Its stated purpose is an executive session on selecting and hiring a Zoning Officer, and the board may take official action afterward. The notice lists both 9:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m., so check with the township before going. *([Notice (PDF)](https://waynetwpwarrencooh.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Zoning-Inspector-Special-Meeting-Notice.pdf))*
