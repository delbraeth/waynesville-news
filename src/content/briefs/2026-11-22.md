## This morning in Waynesville

1. **A first state semifinal, Friday vs. Ironton.** Fresh off Friday's 27-0 regional final win over Bishop Fenwick, Waynesville (11-2) plays Region 19 champion Ironton (12-1) in a Division V state semifinal at 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 27, at Teays Valley High School in Ashville. It is the first state semifinal in school history; the winner plays for the state title Dec. 4. OHSAA says playoff tickets go on sale Tuesdays at ohsaa.org/tickets. *([semifinal preview](/stories/2026-11-21-state-semifinal-preview-ironton/) · [OHSAA pairings](https://www.ohsaa.org/news-media/articles/ohsaa-announces-football-state-semifinal-bracket-pairings-45))*
2. **Last day in Stars Hollow.** Town Square Days wraps up downtown today, 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., with a mostly sunny forecast and a high near 69, unusually mild for late November. *([event map](/images/events/2026-11-21-town-square-days-map.webp))*
3. **Thanksgiving week.** Wayne Local is closed Monday through Friday for Thanksgiving break, and the weather turns sharply colder, with a low near 28 Monday night. MorningStar Baptist Church's outreach ministry is still taking requests for free Thanksgiving food baskets for Waynesville families: text 937-270-2330, email Outreach@morningstardayton.org or message the church on Facebook. *([MorningStar Baptist Church](https://morningstardayton.org/) · [Wayne Local Schools](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
