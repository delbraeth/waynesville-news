## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Semifinal tickets go on sale Tuesday.** Waynesville (11-2) plays Region 19 champion Ironton (12-1) in a Division V state semifinal at 7 p.m. Friday at Teays Valley High School in Ashville, the first state semifinal in school history. OHSAA says playoff tickets go on sale Tuesdays at ohsaa.org/tickets; the winner plays for the state title Dec. 4. *([semifinal preview](/stories/2026-11-21-state-semifinal-preview-ironton/) · [OHSAA pairings](https://www.ohsaa.org/news-media/articles/ohsaa-announces-football-state-semifinal-bracket-pairings-45))*
2. **Cold and sunny, then a wet Thanksgiving.** Today is sunny with a high near 49 and a low near 27 tonight, Tuesday stays sunny and colder with a high of 45, and the National Weather Service forecasts light rain on Thanksgiving Day with a high near 48. No weather alerts are in effect. *([National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891))*
3. **Light shows open this week.** A Carillon Christmas in Dayton opens Tuesday, Light Up Middletown starts Thanksgiving night, and on Friday Yuletide Village's Season of Lights opens at Renaissance Park, along with WinterFest at Kings Island and Clifton Mill's light display. The Christmas Ranch on Waynesville Road is already open and closes Thanksgiving Day. Hillsboro's Winter Wonderland now has its $25-per-vehicle passes on sale for its Nov. 27 opening. *([Holiday Lights guide](/holiday-lights/) · [Yuletide Village](https://yuletidevillage.com/) · [The Christmas Ranch](https://www.thechristmasranch.com/))*
