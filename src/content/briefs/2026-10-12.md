## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Fog this morning, then a warm, sunny stretch.** The National Weather Service has a Dense Fog Advisory in effect until 11:00 a.m.; allow extra time and use low beams if you're driving early. Skies turn mostly sunny later, with a high near 81°. Tuesday looks sunny and warmer, near 86°, before showers and thunderstorms move in Wednesday and Thursday. *([NWS](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891))*
2. **No school today; back Tuesday.** Wayne Local students are off Monday, October 12, for fall break and return Tuesday, October 13, the district reminded families. It's a short week: Friday is a two-hour early release to end the quarter. *([Wayne Local Schools](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
3. **Early voting continues.** The November 3 general election is 22 days away. Early in-person voting is open today from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at the Warren County Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr., Lebanon. Countywide, the ballot includes a Mental Health Recovery Board renewal levy and a Warren County Senior Services renewal levy, plus a statewide constitutional amendment on photo ID for voting. *([Board of Elections schedule](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
