## This morning in Waynesville

1. **A big Spartan weekend.** The Waynesville boys cross country team finished 7th as a team at Saturday's OHSAA state meet, and its top runner placed 28th overall in 16:08.8, according to Wayne Local Schools. The football team, fresh off Friday's 35-13 playoff win over Preble Shawnee, plays a regional semifinal at Graham Local in St. Paris this Friday at 7:00 p.m. *([Wayne Local Schools](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools) · [next game](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d))*
2. **Last day of Hearthwarming Holidays.** Downtown shops are open noon–5 p.m. today for the final day of the Merchants Association's holiday shopping weekend. American Legion Post 615 holds its annual Veterans Day dinner today starting at 3:00 p.m. *([Merchants Association](https://waynesvilleshops.com/?p=17396) · [events calendar](/events/))*
3. **Roads plan comes to the village Tuesday.** Warren County Engineer Kurt Weber will discuss the North Warren County Transportation Plan Tuesday at 7:00 p.m. at the Village of Waynesville office, 1400 Lytle Rd., according to the Chamber, which asks attendees to RSVP. The plan's online survey is open through November 17. *([Chamber](https://www.waynesvilleohio.com/events) · [survey](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/pages/f27bc61005b842439ceb569b82c0b9cf))*
