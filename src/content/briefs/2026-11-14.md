## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Fantasy Festival downtown, 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.** The free third annual festival brings LARP archery, falcon encounters, fire performers and live music to High Street, plus six food trucks. In the Dwarven Beer Garden, Sean Nos plays at 11 a.m. and 5 p.m. and Creep It Real at 12:30 and 3:30 p.m.; Riddles in the Dark is at 3 p.m. at the Stone House Tavern, and the 5 p.m. Whiskey Witches bourbon tasting inside the Villager Pub requires preregistration. *([festival map](/images/events/2026-11-14-fantasy-festival-map.png))*
2. **Waynesville 14, Graham 7.** The Spartans won Friday's Division V, Region 20 semifinal in St. Paris and meet Bishop Fenwick in the regional final at 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 20, at a neutral site OHSAA has not yet announced. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d))*
3. **School board weighs a levy.** At Monday's meeting, board members discussed whether to place a levy on the May or November ballot, and Board President Amanda Garrett proposed an exploratory committee that would include staff, according to the district's Board Meeting Recap. A May issue would require steps to be completed by February, and Treasurer Carolyn Huber reported the projected General Fund year-end balance falling from $8.4 million in FY2026 to $7.1 million in FY2027. *([Wayne Local Board Meeting Recap, Nov. 12](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*

## Schools
The board also heard that revenue is running about $447,800 above forecast, accepted $6,748.88 in donations, and recognized eight students and four staff members with Spartan Recognition Awards. Radium Girls plays tonight at 7 and Sunday at 2 at the Performing Arts Center, and the board next meets Dec. 14 at 6 p.m. *([Wayne Local Board Meeting Recap, Nov. 12](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
