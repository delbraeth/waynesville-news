## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Spartan boys cross country qualifies for State.** Wayne Local's boys cross country team finished third at Saturday's regional meet and qualified as a team for the state meet, the district reports. One Spartan runner also placed 10th and qualified individually. *([district post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid02N8mBydzcM1khuw9EomS5xD2AsBH5pAp2QqaPabJsWmENb6KHGpHq1poDJZqpbayal))*
2. **Today is the last day to vote early.** Early in-person voting is open from 1:00 to 5:00 p.m. at the Warren County Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr., Lebanon. On Election Day, Tuesday, November 3, polls are open 6:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Countywide, the ballot carries renewal levies for the Mental Health Recovery Board and Warren County Senior Services, plus a statewide constitutional amendment on photo ID for voting. *([Board of Elections notice](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
3. **Final day for the Renaissance Festival and Fall Fest.** The Ohio Renaissance Festival ends its season with the last day of Heroes and Villains Weekend, 10:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., at Renaissance Park ([festival](https://renfestival.com/)). Waynesville Fall Fest at 7392 E. State Route 73 is open noon to 6:00 p.m. for its closing day. *([Fall Fest](https://waynesvillefallfest.com/))* Daylight saving time ended at 2:00 a.m., so remember to set clocks back an hour.
