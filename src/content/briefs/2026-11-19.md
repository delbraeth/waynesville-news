## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Spartans play for the region title Friday.** Waynesville meets Bishop Fenwick in the Division V, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. Friday at Lakota East High School in Liberty Township. The all-village send-off starts at 4:30 p.m. outside the high school; buses head down Main Street to State Route 73. *([send-off flyer](/images/events/2026-11-20-spartan-send-off.webp) · [game details](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/bishop-fenwick-franklin-vs-waynesville/11-20-2026/?c=2f2a6d51-5706-4af5-a1bd-43c71e2f70d3))*
2. **Stars Hollow weekend downtown.** Town Square Days runs 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday and Sunday, rain or shine, presented by the Waynesville Merchant Association. *([flyer](/images/events/2026-11-21-stars-hollow-town-square-days.png))*
3. **Nights of Lights opens Friday in Lebanon.** The drive-through show at the Warren County Fairgrounds runs nightly through Jan. 2 by timed ticket, $30 per carload of up to eight, sold online. *([Holiday Lights guide](/holiday-lights/))*
