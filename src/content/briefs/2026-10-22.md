## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Fun Run raises $68,785 for Waynesville Elementary.** The district announced the final total for the 2026 Waynesville Elementary Fun Run: $68,785, including student-collected donations, and thanked students, teachers, staff, families, community members and the Waynesville Elementary PTO. *([Wayne Local Schools on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid0bTxy3h6pieue6aCbXNyFbhVn8kLFG5GPePS1Y4Nn8FkwTxoCdhFgqFytaAc5eBnDl))*
2. **PSAT day at WHS; Supper Club wants community adults.** Waynesville High students who registered take the PSAT today; the district reminded them to charge their Chromebooks. The high school is also looking for adults to join its Supper Club on Thursday, November 5, 6:00 to 7:30 p.m., an evening pairing students in grades 9–12 with community adults to talk careers and experiences over a meal; see the district's post for how to sign up. *([PSAT post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid02b776tzgoosLyKswiumZqEpQoGro6iehSyFPChzQEBrSurH2SnQeybCWekDBBeSe8l) · [Supper Club post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid034X6UHAbQGGLAeQW7nJiMATgjhHv3EMnoc54vGw1yhsLTHjpxvx9CLgmi5HNXKiChl))*
3. **Football closes the regular season at Valley View Friday.** Coming off a 35-7 home win over Eaton, the Spartans play at Valley View Friday at 7:00 p.m. Girls soccer hosts Indian Lake tonight at 7:00 p.m., and boys soccer, 6-1 winners over Northwestern Tuesday, hosts Madison Saturday at 7:00 p.m. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/valley-view-germantown-vs-waynesville/10-23-2026/?c=09d38f0b-3343-491c-8a5a-5266caf752f2))*

## Schools
