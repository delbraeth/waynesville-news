Frost is possible overnight, with a low near 30° after a high of 55°, so bring a layer. *([game details](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/preble-shawnee-camden-vs-waynesville/11-6-2026/?c=a24a818e-b5b2-4c28-ba36-48a9b46daf00))*

The Warren County Engineer presents the plan at the Village of Waynesville office, 1400 Lytle Rd., Tuesday at 7:00 p.m., and the plan's online survey is open through November 17. *([Dayton Daily News](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMilAFBVV95cUxPdnRqdHhfSkFMdndPYldKeGtlcG16ZjlUVUw0cWlnRlpwSWthcExrNVB6a3Z4S0ZrM2JWaGRqMXNGbEpOUHhIaWpHU3VjZE9SR2huaW4tYkdCWlFDYUF3bm1TWU5VSE93M3g3SUliQUl3X3dHWTUzQTA4Q1J0QXl2MW82NWlON2hIN3g1VW9QUl9DRWV0?oc=5) · [online survey](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/pages/f27bc61005b842439ceb569b82c0b9cf))*
