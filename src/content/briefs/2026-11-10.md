## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Roads plan comes to the village tonight.** Warren County Engineer Kurt Weber will discuss the North Warren County Transportation Plan at 7:00 p.m. at the Village of Waynesville office, 1400 Lytle Rd., according to the Chamber, which asks attendees to RSVP. Local 12 reports planners have drawn six proposals for a better I-71/I-75 connection across the northern county; any construction is years away, and the plan's online survey closes November 17. *([Chamber](https://www.waynesvilleohio.com/events) · [Local 12](https://local12.com/news/local/warren-county-seeks-input-on-proposals-improve-i-71-i-75-connection-regional-transportation-plan-construction-building-cost-price-traffic-detour-cars-vehicles-semi-trucks-lebanon-cities-roads-advantages-issues-wilmington-road-cincinnati) · [survey](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/pages/f27bc61005b842439ceb569b82c0b9cf))*
2. **Wayne Local salutes veterans Wednesday.** The district invites veterans and active-duty military members to a Veterans Day breakfast from 7:45 to 8:30 a.m., a program from 8:50 to 9:30 a.m. and a veterans car parade from 9:30 to 10:00 a.m., and asks guests to RSVP, according to its Facebook page. Wayne Township's administrative offices will be closed Wednesday for the holiday. *([district post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid02PFJzSEid65LiQnTUUn97uZ57X3hJccWm59CPnw2fKEduqBjQb4PhGpBC4Rktm2Anl) · [township notice](https://waynetwpwarrencooh.gov/veterans-day-observation/))*
3. **Spartans head to Graham Friday.** Fresh off a 35-13 playoff win over Preble Shawnee, Spartan football plays a regional semifinal at Graham Local in St. Paris Friday at 7:00 p.m. Back home Saturday, the Waynesville Fantasy Festival fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. *([next game](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d) · [events calendar](/events/))*
