## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Voters renew both county levies.** Warren County Senior Services' 1.21-mill renewal (Issue 7) passed with 67.7% and the Mental Health Recovery Board's 1-mill renewal (Issue 6) with 63.9%, with all precincts reporting, according to unofficial results reported by the [Warren County Post](https://warrencountypost.com/g/lebanon-oh/n/397174/warren-county-voters-renew-mental-health-and-senior-services-levies). Statewide, the photo-ID amendment (Issue 3) passed by a 2-1 margin, the [Statehouse News Bureau reports](https://www.statenews.org/government-politics/2026-11-04/amendment-requiring-photo-id-for-in-person-voting-in-ohio-passes-easily). *([official results](https://liveresults.boe.ohio.gov/ENR/warrenohenr/18/en/R_Index_18.html))*
2. **Spartans host Preble Shawnee; school board meets Monday.** Varsity football plays Preble Shawnee at home Friday at 7:00 p.m. The Board of Education's regular meeting, with Spartan Recognition Awards, is Monday, November 9, at 6:00 p.m. in the WLS Performing Arts Center. *([game details](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/preble-shawnee-camden-vs-waynesville/11-6-2026/?c=a24a818e-b5b2-4c28-ba36-48a9b46daf00) · [district](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
3. **Hearthwarming Holidays opens Friday.** The downtown holiday shopping weekend runs Friday through Sunday, November 6–8; check the event page for each day's hours. *([Merchants Association](https://waynesvilleshops.com/event/hearthwarming-holidays-in-waynesville-ohio/))*
