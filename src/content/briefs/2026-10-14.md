1. **Quarter ends Friday with Senior Night.** Wayne Local's first quarter closes Friday with a two-hour early release. 2. **Storms today, then a sharp cool-down.** A slight chance of storms this morning becomes showers and thunderstorms later, with a high near 82°. Thursday brings a chance of rain (high 73°), Friday rain showers and a high of just 64°, and Saturday turns sunny but chilly: high 56°, with lows near 38° Friday night and 34° Saturday night. No NWS alerts are in effect this morning. *([NWS](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891))*

Clarksville Rd. Its stated purpose is an executive session on the selection and hiring of a Zoning Officer, and the board may take official action in open session afterward.
