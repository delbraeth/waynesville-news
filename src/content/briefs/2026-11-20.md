## This morning in Waynesville


1. **Spartans play for the region title tonight.** Waynesville meets Bishop Fenwick in the Division V, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. at Lakota East High School in Liberty Township. The all-village send-off starts at 4:30 p.m. at the high school; the buses turn onto Main Street and then State Route 73, and the athletic department says Spartans towels will be available at the game. *([send-off flyer](/images/events/2026-11-20-spartan-send-off.webp) · [game details](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/bishop-fenwick-franklin-vs-waynesville/11-20-2026/?c=2f2a6d51-5706-4af5-a1bd-43c71e2f70d3) · [Spartans Athletics](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid0K6WVUdk3NzrfJAD6ANQ7R5zZuaGTo7RJuFgvXYWVzhWFmuwNc88vHNcrsCHecb2Sl))*
2. **Stars Hollow weekend downtown.** Town Square Days runs 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday and 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday, rain or shine. Main Street is closed between North and Miami streets; shuttle parking is at the Waynesville Performing Arts Center, 659 Dayton Rd. *([event map](/images/events/2026-11-21-town-square-days-map.webp))*
3. **Library holiday auction opens at noon.** The Friends of the Mary L. Cook Library auction takes in-person bids on donated gift baskets and decorations through 7 p.m. Dec. 10, with proceeds supporting the library's free programs. The Letters to Santa mailbox is also up at the library through Dec. 15. *([library](https://www.mlcook.lib.oh.us/))*
