## This morning in Waynesville

1. **School board meets tonight at 6:00 p.m.** The Wayne Local Board of Education holds its regular October meeting in the Performing Arts Center (second floor) at 625 Dayton Rd. It's also Boys Soccer Senior Night and a Volleyball Orange Out at the high school: varsity volleyball hosts Fairmont at 6:30 p.m. and varsity boys soccer hosts Legacy Christian Academy at 7:15 p.m. *([district post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid032ia7N3EsLqiKJCQT8DHfAD6EckXpXbooW4sKXuPhpuGyVx4Hw3RdBfY6ZjA3SYCnl); [BoardDocs](https://go.boarddocs.com/oh/waylo/Board.nsf/Public))*
2. **Today is the last day to register to vote.** The Warren County Board of Elections is open 8:00 a.m.-9:00 p.m. today for registrations and address updates for the November 3 general election, now 29 days away. Early in-person voting begins Tuesday, October 6, at the Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr, Lebanon. *([election notice](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
3. **Settlers Grove street-lighting hearing Tuesday.** Wayne Township Trustees meet Tuesday at 7:00 p.m., and at 7:30 p.m. will hold a public hearing on a petition to create a street-lighting district for the Settlers Grove subdivision under Chapter 515 of the Ohio Revised Code, at the township administration building, 6050 N. Clarksville Rd. *([township notice](https://waynetwpwarrencooh.gov/notice-of-public-hearing-proposed-settlers-grove-street-lighting-district/))*
