## This morning in Waynesville

1. Wayne Local's first full week of the 2026-27 school year wraps up — the district launched **My Ride K-12**, a new app for families to track bus pickup/drop-off times, following last Sunday's Back-to-School Bash at Bicentennial Park. [My Ride K-12](https://www.wayne-local.com/district-news/entry/wls-introduces-my-ride-k-12-transportation-app/) · [Back-to-School Bash recap](https://www.wayne-local.com/district-news/entry/back-to-school-bash-sunday-august-16/)
2. Warren County Commissioners are back in session Tuesday, Aug. 25 at 9 AM — the next open look at county business. [agenda](https://commissioners.warrencountyohio.gov/News/AgendaMinutes/Index)
3. Warren County Park District has added a new **Hisey Fall Fest** to its Oct. 3 lineup at Hisey Park, joining the Ohio Renaissance Festival's Sept. 5 season opener and the Waynesville Airshow & Fly-in the same Labor Day weekend. [Hisey Fall Fest](https://www.warrencountyparks.com/events/) · [Renaissance Festival](https://renfestival.com/) · [Airshow](https://www.daytonlocal.com/events/waynesville-labor-day-weekend-airshow/)
