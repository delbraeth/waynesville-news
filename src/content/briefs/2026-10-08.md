## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Conferences tonight, then a long weekend.** Wayne Local parent-teacher conferences run today from 4:00 to 7:00 p.m. at the elementary, middle and high schools. There is no school for students Friday, October 9 (teacher professional day) or Monday, October 12 (fall break), per the district's October community newsletter. *([wayne-local.com](https://www.wayne-local.com/))*
2. **Pink Out Friday night.** Waynesville hosts Madison at 7:00 p.m. Friday at Spartan Community Field in the WHS Pink Out game for breast cancer awareness. The district asks fans to wear pink "to honor survivors, support those currently fighting, and remember loved ones." *([district post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid04qSGGC2x9FmTyTrC8FRjbxuPYWB5jcACSxQhZfyLMJRWndt8zfPRnoXBEY8Lh8Xrl))*
3. **Sauerkraut Festival this weekend.** The 56th Ohio Sauerkraut Festival runs downtown Saturday, October 10, from 9:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. and Sunday, October 11, from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., per the Chamber. The forecast is a high near 79° and partly sunny Saturday, with showers and thunderstorms in the forecast Sunday. *([Chamber](https://www.waynesvilleohio.com/events))*
