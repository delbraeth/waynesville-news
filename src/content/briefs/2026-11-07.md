## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Spartans advance in the playoffs.** Waynesville beat Preble Shawnee 35-13 Friday night at Spartan Community Field. Next up is a regional semifinal at Graham Local in St. Paris, Friday at 7:00 p.m. *([box score](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/preble-shawnee-camden-vs-waynesville/11-6-2026/?c=a24a818e-b5b2-4c28-ba36-48a9b46daf00) · [next game](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/graham-local-st-paris-vs-waynesville/11-13-2026/?c=3a9be22b-749e-4a99-a948-867beeb7242d))*
2. **Boys cross country finishes 7th at state.** *Updated Saturday evening:* The Waynesville boys cross country team placed 7th as a team at the OHSAA state meet, and its top runner finished 28th overall in 16:08.8, according to Wayne Local Schools. *([Wayne Local Schools](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools))*
3. **Hearthwarming Holidays continues downtown.** Shops are open 11 a.m.–8 p.m. today and noon–5 p.m. Sunday for the Merchants Association's holiday shopping weekend. The CrossView Christmas Bazaar at CrossView Christian Church, 4237 E.
