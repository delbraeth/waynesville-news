## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Weigh in on a possible new I-75/I-71 connection.** The Warren County Engineer's Office and the Warren County Transportation Improvement District, working with regional planners OKI and the Miami Valley Regional Planning Commission, hold a public open house Wednesday, October 28, from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. at the Warren County Career Center, 3525 S.R. 48, Lebanon. Drop in anytime. The North Warren County Transportation Plan's study area takes in Waynesville, Corwin and Wayne Township. The project team will show several general locations being studied for a new connection between I-75 and I-71, which the county stresses are not final routes, along with 10 roadway locations it has identified as high priorities for improvement. "We're aware of the lack of connections between I-75 and I-71 and safety concerns on key roadways and this study is the first step towards addressing the situation," County Engineer Kurt Weber said. The same materials go online Wednesday on the project website, where the comment survey is open October 28 through November 17; comments can also go to the Engineer's Office at kweber@warrencountyoh.gov or 210 W. Main St., Lebanon, OH 45036, and must be received by November 17. *([project website](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/) · Warren County Engineer's Office news release)*

   ![Map of the North Warren County Transportation Plan study area, covering northern Warren County from Carlisle and Franklin east to Waynesville, Corwin, Harveysburg and Clarksville, and from Springboro south to Lebanon](/images/2026-10-27-north-warren-study-area.jpg)
   *The study area. Map: North Warren County Transportation Plan.*
