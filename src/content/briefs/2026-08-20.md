## Schools

Wayne Local Schools rolled out **My Ride K-12**, a new transportation app giving families access to their student's bus pickup/drop-off times and stop notifications ([details](https://www.wayne-local.com/district-news/entry/wls-introduces-my-ride-k-12-transportation-app/)), alongside **updated bus stop locations for 2026-27** ([details](https://www.wayne-local.com/district-news/entry/new-bus-stop-locations-for-2026-27/)). Heading into the year, Waynesville Elementary also welcomed **Ben Sparks** as its new assistant principal, coming from 11 years as a K–5 PE teacher in Fairborn ([details](https://www.wayne-local.com/district-news/entry/wls-welcomes-ben-sparks-as-wes-assistant-principal/)).
