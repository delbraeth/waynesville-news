## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Spartans' historic season ends in the state semifinal.** Ironton beat Waynesville 35-10 Friday night in the Division V state semifinal at Teays Valley High School in Ashville, ending a season in which the Spartans reached the state final four for the first time in program history after winning the Region 20 title with a 27-0 shutout of Bishop Fenwick. "Congratulations to the Spartans on a phenomenal season!" Spartans Athletics posted after the game, and Wayne Local Schools added, "We're so proud of you!" *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/ironton-vs-waynesville/11-27-2026/?c=ed4a7889-09b4-4b7e-8729-17891320fd62) · [Spartans Athletics](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid02sAuZsmeXAM5PPbGc15kgnyeLKpHUaaTerqtBXDoTirzjV6G1cmD5ExNkuU4MnriPl) · [Wayne Local Schools](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid0iZbkbdYBvhZzJunuorSonmL6XMaufF2pCJsQoJKS3Pt8dd7JvUCgeHd8uXvxBhSTl))*
2. **Holiday lights tonight, before Sunday's rain.** Yuletide Village: Season of Lights at Renaissance Park, 10542 E. State Route 73, is open tonight from 3 to 9 p.m., its Saturday hours through Dec. 19, with tickets sold online. Today's partly sunny forecast makes it the better night of the weekend for light displays; the National Weather Service calls for rain showers Sunday. Our Holiday Lights guide lists a dozen displays in and around Waynesville with dates, hours and prices. *([Holiday Lights guide](/holiday-lights/) · [Yuletide Village](https://yuletidevillage.com/))*
3. **Red Lion-Five Points Road closes Monday.** The Warren County Engineer will close Red Lion-Five Points Road at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks starting Monday, Nov. 30, weather permitting, to repair the bridge guardrail. The detour uses Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73. *([County Engineer](https://engineer.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Information/NewsReleases/2026/20261130_2_Week_Closure_of_Red_Lion_5_Points_Rd.pdf))*
