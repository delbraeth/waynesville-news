## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Open house today on a possible new I-75/I-71 connection.** The North Warren County Transportation Plan open house runs from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m. today at the Warren County Career Center, 3525 S.R. 48, Lebanon; drop in anytime. The study area takes in Waynesville, Corwin and Wayne Township, and the project team will show several general locations being studied for a new link between I-75 and I-71 (the county stresses these are not final routes) along with 10 roadway locations it has flagged as high priorities for improvement. The same materials go up on the project website today, and the online comment survey is open through November 17. *([project website](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/))*
2. **Early voting today until 7:30 p.m.** The November 3 general election is six days away. Early in-person voting is open today from 7:30 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. at the Warren County Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr., Lebanon. Countywide, the ballot carries renewal levies for the Mental Health Recovery Board and Warren County Senior Services, plus a statewide constitutional amendment on photo ID for voting. *([Board of Elections notice](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
3. **Halloween week, Wednesday edition.** Ohio Living Quaker Heights, 514 High St., opens its doors for trick-or-treat tonight from 6:00 to 7:00 p.m., and the Chamber's Bourbon & Cigars mixer is at the Villager Pub, 144 S. Main St., from 5:00 to 8:00 p.m., open to members and non-members alike. Village-wide trick-or-treat is Saturday, October 31, from 6:00 to 8:00 p.m. *([Chamber events](https://www.waynesvilleohio.com/events))*
