## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Send the Spartans off this afternoon.** The All-Village Spartan Send-Off starts when the buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 p.m. today; the route turns right out of school, right on U.S. 42 and left on State Route 73, and fans are asked to line it in orange and black. Waynesville, 11-2 and the Region 20 champion, plays Region 19 champion Ironton, 12-1, at 7 p.m. at Teays Valley High School in Ashville in the first state semifinal in Waynesville history. Tickets are $16 for adults and $10 for students online at ohsaa.org/tickets, the gate takes cash only, parking is $5, and only bags 5 by 8 by 1 inches or smaller are allowed. The National Weather Service forecast for Ashville tonight calls for a chance of rain before midnight, a low around 32 and wind gusts up to 28 mph. Spectrum is streaming the game, and the winner plays for the state title Friday, Dec. 4. *([send-off flyer](/images/events/2026-11-27-spartan-send-off.webp) · [game-day flyer](/images/events/2026-11-27-semifinal-game-day.webp) · [Spartans Athletics](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid0CzTD3Kb4qvEQMRpVKLWLgyDw7rqjXSAtHA4HphMKJ5yQE9vr2S8bCjDRWGDG3NT9l) · [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/ironton-vs-waynesville/11-27-2026/?c=ed4a7889-09b4-4b7e-8729-17891320fd62))*
2. **Holiday lights season opens tonight.** Yuletide Village: Season of Lights opens tonight at Renaissance Park, 10542 E. State Route 73, and runs Fridays 5 to 9 p.m. and Saturdays 3 to 9 p.m. through Dec. 19, with tickets sold online; its website lists a Black Friday sale today on $22 any-day adult tickets. WinterFest at Kings Island in Mason, the Clifton Mill light display and Hillsboro's Winter Wonderland also open tonight. Our Holiday Lights guide lists a dozen displays in and around Waynesville with dates, hours and prices, plus the season's holiday events. *([Holiday Lights guide](/holiday-lights/) · [Yuletide Village](https://yuletidevillage.com/))*
3. **Red Lion-Five Points Road closes Monday.** The Warren County Engineer will close Red Lion-Five Points Road at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks starting Monday, Nov. 30, weather permitting, to repair the bridge guardrail. The detour uses Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73. *([County Engineer](https://engineer.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Information/NewsReleases/2026/20261130_2_Week_Closure_of_Red_Lion_5_Points_Rd.pdf))*
