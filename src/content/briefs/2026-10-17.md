## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Spartans beat Eaton 35-7 on Senior Night.** Waynesville's varsity football team rolled past Eaton 35-7 at home Friday night on a USA Night and Senior Night that honored seniors in football, cheer, band, cross country and boys golf. The JV plays at Eaton this morning at 10:00, and the varsity travels to Valley View next Friday at 7:00 p.m. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/eaton-vs-waynesville/10-16-2026/?c=ae4a8cf1-eaf5-4c11-8eeb-35412dd55d0b) · [Wayne Local Schools](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid0222AvA382iHuom4kkMM7SZN82vQEcXgXqGFouuFMDwBRtP9Y79uXPpaWRRewBR42fl))*
2. **Freeze warning through 9 a.m. Sunday.** The National Weather Service in Wilmington has a Freeze Warning in effect until 9:00 a.m. Sunday. Today is sunny but cold, with a high of 55° and a low near 31° tonight, and the forecast calls for widespread frost Sunday morning before a sunny afternoon near 63°. Cover or bring in tender plants this evening, and dress warmly for tonight's outdoor events. *([NWS](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891))*
3. **Early voting resumes Monday.** With the November 3 general election 17 days away, early in-person voting resumes Monday, October 19, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at the Warren County Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr., Lebanon. Countywide, the ballot includes renewal levies for the Mental Health Recovery Board and Warren County Senior Services, plus a statewide constitutional amendment on photo ID for voting. *([Board of Elections schedule](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf) · [issues](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/CandIssues/UpcomingIssues/Index))*
