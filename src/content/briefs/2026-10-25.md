## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Bull rider dies after Fox Hollow Rodeo injury.** A bull rider died after being critically injured in the bullpen at the Fox Hollow Rodeo in Waynesville on Saturday evening, WLWT reports. Clearcreek Township police told the station the call came in around 7:41 p.m.; the rider was taken to a hospital and later died. The rider's identity has not been released. *([WLWT](https://www.wlwt.com/article/bull-rider-dies-after-incident-at-fox-hollow-rodeo/74239481))*
2. **Cross country heads to regionals.** The Waynesville boys cross country team finished as district runner-up, and both the boys and girls teams advance to regionals next weekend, the district reports. *([Wayne Local Schools on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid0ZFePzVr3FDeTosUxWpsaXNoPmFnFsDwLeCSa8sanNPakHjUdeuhXyjcGaJMHmMAfl))*
3. **Trick-or-treat downtown this afternoon.** Participating shops on Main, High and Miami streets and Scioto Square hand out treats today from 1:00 to 4:00 p.m., under sunny skies and a forecast high of 73°. Myths & Monsters Weekend wraps up today at the Ohio Renaissance Festival, 10:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m., and CrossView Christian Church hosts a Trunk or Treat this evening, 5:00 to 7:00 p.m. *([Trick-or-Treat Guide](/trick-or-treat/) · [Merchants Association](https://waynesvilleshops.com/event/waynesville-trick-or-treat-in-historic-downtown/))*
