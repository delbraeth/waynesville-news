## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Volleyball Senior Night tonight against Carlisle.** Waynesville High honors its volleyball seniors as the Spartans host Carlisle, with JV at 5:30 p.m. and varsity at 6:30 p.m. Varsity comes in off a 3-1 home win over Batavia on Saturday. The WHS and WMS choirs follow with their fall concert Wednesday, October 7, at 7:00 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/volleyball/match/carlisle-vs-waynesville/10-6-2026/?c=4bc6c3fe-5c85-4d86-8de4-4b9a91844710); [district post](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools/posts/pfbid027B5DNDEg4mVf4UFvmU8z5TauZgpgbwCoYbthwG8JKevDGWPP56CnWb9t3VxHbhK1l))*
2. **Early in-person voting opens today.** Voters can cast ballots for the November 3 general election, now 28 days away, from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at the Warren County Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr, Lebanon. Countywide, the ballot includes a statewide constitutional amendment requiring photo ID for voting and renewal levies for the Mental Health Recovery Board and Warren County Senior Services. *([election notice](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf); [issues](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/CandIssues/UpcomingIssues/Index))*
3. **Settlers Grove street-lighting hearing tonight.** Wayne Township Trustees meet at 7:00 p.m. At 7:30 p.m. they hold a public hearing on a petition to create a street-lighting district for the Settlers Grove subdivision under Chapter 515 of the Ohio Revised Code. Both are at the township administration building, 6050 N. Clarksville Rd. *([township notice](https://waynetwpwarrencooh.gov/notice-of-public-hearing-proposed-settlers-grove-street-lighting-district/))*
