## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Frost advisory until 9 a.m., then sunshine all week.** The National Weather Service in Wilmington has a Frost Advisory in effect until 9:00 a.m. today. After that, the forecast calls for sun with a high near 69° today and highs of 69° Tuesday through Thursday, with lows in the low 40s. Village leaf pickup begins today; rake leaves to the curb, not into the street, so they don't clog storm drains. *([NWS](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891) · [Village of Waynesville](https://www.villageofwaynesville.org/news/entry/leaf-pick-up/))*
2. **Village Council tonight, township trustees Tuesday.** Waynesville Village Council meets at 7:00 p.m. today; the draft minutes in its agenda packet record that council adopted Ordinance 2026-043 as an emergency at its previous meeting, 7-0, authorizing a village credit card account and Insured Cash Sweep enrollment with Peoples Bank. The Wayne Township Trustees meet Tuesday at 7:00 p.m. at 6050 N. Clarksville Rd., and the village Finance Committee meeting has been moved to 6:00 p.m. October 26 at 1400 Lytle Road. *([council agenda packet](https://www.villageofwaynesville.org/media/site/meetings/Council%20Agenda%2010.19.2026.pdf) · [township agenda](https://waynetwpwarrencooh.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Trustees-Meeting-Agenda-20261015.pdf) · [Finance Committee notice](https://www.villageofwaynesville.org/news/entry/finance-committee-meeting-rescheduled/))*
3. **County budget work begins, and a Little Miami sewer plant repair is on the agenda.** The Warren County Commissioners' Tuesday agenda includes an emergency repair to aerator blower #1 at the Lower Little Miami Wastewater Treatment Plant, a continued public hearing on a 30-acre Union Township rezoning, and a 2027 budget work session at 10:30 a.m. Early in-person voting for the November 3 election continues today, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., at the Board of Elections in Lebanon. *([Session 1 agenda](https://commissioners.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/AgendaMinutes/Agenda%5C2026%5C102026%20Regular%20Session%201.pdf) · [Session 2 agenda](https://commissioners.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/AgendaMinutes/Agenda%5C2026%5C102026%20Regular%20Session%202.pdf) · [Board of Elections schedule](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
4. **Spartans lock up a playoff spot.** Friday's 35-7 Senior Night win over Eaton moved Waynesville to 7-2. Joe Eitel's unofficial Harbin playoff-points tracker now ranks the Spartans third in Division V, Region 20 and shows them as having clinched a playoff berth; the top 12 teams in the region qualify. OHSAA is scheduled to announce qualifiers Sunday, Oct. 25. The regular season ends Friday at Valley View, 7:00 p.m. *([Region 20 ratings (unofficial)](https://joeeitel.com/hsfoot/rankings/2026/region-20) · [MaxPreps recap](https://www.maxpreps.com/news/GPghTvgnF0a7tPJCHPhB0Q/football-recap-waynesville-skates-past-eaton-with-ease--how-to-watch.htm))*
