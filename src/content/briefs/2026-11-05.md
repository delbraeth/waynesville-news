## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Holiday shopping weekend starts Friday.** Hearthwarming Holidays runs downtown Friday through Sunday, November 6–8, with shops open 11 a.m.–8 p.m. Friday and Saturday and noon–5 p.m. Sunday. Nearby, the CrossView Christmas Bazaar at CrossView Christian Church, 4237 E. Social Row Rd., is Friday 3–7 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m.–4 p.m. *([Merchants Association](https://waynesvilleshops.com/event/hearthwarming-holidays-in-waynesville-ohio/) · [events calendar](/events/))*
2. **Spartans host Preble Shawnee Friday.** Varsity football plays Preble Shawnee at home Friday at 7:00 p.m. *([game details](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/preble-shawnee-camden-vs-waynesville/11-6-2026/?c=a24a818e-b5b2-4c28-ba36-48a9b46daf00))*
3. **County engineer comes to the village Tuesday.** The Warren County Engineer will talk about the North Warren County Transportation Plan and what's next for Waynesville's roads Tuesday, November 10, at 7:00 p.m. at the Village of Waynesville office, 1400 Lytle Rd. The plan's online survey is open through November 17. *([Chamber calendar](https://www.waynesvilleohio.com/events) · [online survey](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/pages/f27bc61005b842439ceb569b82c0b9cf))*
