## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Wear orange Wednesday; band's Goodwill drive ends Thursday.** Wayne Local students and staff will wear orange Wednesday, October 21, for Unity Day, part of National Bullying Prevention Month, the district says. The band's Goodwill "Drive to Victory" runs through Thursday: drop off clothing, household items, toys, small working appliances and more in the Performing Arts Center parking lot, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. *([Wayne Local Schools on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/waynelocalschools))*
2. **Data centers on the township trustees' agenda tonight.** The Wayne Township Trustees meet at 7:00 p.m. at 6050 N. Clarksville Rd. The posted agenda lists "Data Centers" and the "Old Road Garage" under old business, a presentation from the Warren County Elderly Services Program, and a resolution revising street lighting plans for the Settlers Grove subdivision. *([township agenda](https://waynetwpwarrencooh.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/10.20.26-agenda.pdf))*
3. **Sunny all week; 14 days to Election Day.** The National Weather Service forecasts sun and a high near 72° today, with highs from 68° to 71° through Friday and lows in the 40s; no alerts are in effect. Early in-person voting for the November 3 election continues today, 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., at the Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr., Lebanon, and the last day to request an absentee ballot is Tuesday, October 27. *([NWS](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891) · [Board of Elections schedule](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
