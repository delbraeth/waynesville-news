## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Stars Hollow comes to Main Street this weekend.** Town Square Days runs 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday and Sunday downtown, rain or shine, presented by the Waynesville Merchant Association. Saturday's 11:30 a.m. dance marathon at Turn Out Dance Academy takes two canned goods or a $5 donation for the Waynesville Food Pantry. *([flyer](/images/events/2026-11-21-stars-hollow-town-square-days.png) · [dance marathon](https://turnoutdanceacademy.com/))*
2. **Youth deer gun season this weekend.** Ohio's youth gun season is Nov. 21–22, and Caesar Creek State Park reminds visitors that hunting is permitted in several areas near hiking, mountain bike and horse trails; the park suggests hunter orange in the woods. *([park post](https://www.facebook.com/CaesarCreekStatePark/posts/pfbid028eieik3pboFRSCQyDBuFLHQwpfke43vaiCUg4xjzPxFmJK8Yzrdj6PHkyNkfkw3Cl))*
3. **Library holiday season opens Friday.** Bidding on the Friends of the Library Holiday Auction opens at noon Friday and closes Dec. 10 at 7 p.m.; the Letters to Santa mailbox also opens Friday, and letters dropped by Dec. 15 get a reply. *([Mary L. Cook Public Library](https://www.mlcook.lib.oh.us/))*
