## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Last call for Radium Girls.** Waynesville's high school theater closes its run of the D.W. Gregory play at 2 p.m. today at the Performing Arts Center, 659 Dayton Rd. Tickets are $10 for students and $12 for adults at the door, cash or card. *([flyer](/images/events/2026-11-13-radium-girls.jpg))*
2. **Spartans head to Lakota East.** After Friday's 14-7 win at Graham, Waynesville meets Bishop Fenwick in the Division V, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 20; the game will be played at a neutral site, Lakota East High School in Liberty Township, the Spartans announced Sunday. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/bishop-fenwick-franklin-vs-waynesville/11-20-2026/?c=2f2a6d51-5706-4af5-a1bd-43c71e2f70d3) · [Spartans graphic](/images/events/2026-11-20-regional-final-lakota-east.webp))*
3. **A full week of meetings.** Village Council meets Monday, the county commissioners take up a geotechnical study for the Waynesville wastewater plant project Tuesday morning, and the Wayne Township trustees meet Tuesday at 7 p.m. Details are under Local government.
