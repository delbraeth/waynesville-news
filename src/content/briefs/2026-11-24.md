## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Line the route Friday afternoon.** Spartans Athletics is calling for an All-Village Send-Off on Black Friday: the buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 p.m., turn right out of school, right on U.S. 42 and left on State Route 73, and fans are asked to wear orange and black. Waynesville (11-2) plays Ironton (12-1) in the Division V state semifinal at 7 p.m. at Teays Valley High School in Ashville; OHSAA says playoff tickets go on sale Tuesdays at ohsaa.org/tickets. *([Spartans Athletics](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid0L6PkynSxezpyWQ9n246V3J8KhZX21igtQ5iSmDYwWRDYdq3ThpMUmfMYjdDPM1LXl) · [OHSAA pairings](https://www.ohsaa.org/news-media/articles/ohsaa-announces-football-state-semifinal-bracket-pairings-45))*
2. **Sunny today, wet for the holiday.** The National Weather Service forecasts a sunny high near 44 today, rain on Thanksgiving Day with a high near 48, and rain and snow on Friday with a high near 45. No weather alerts are in effect. *([National Weather Service](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=39.5287&lon=-84.0891))*
3. **Bridge closure on Red Lion-Five Points Road.** The Warren County Engineer says Red Lion-Five Points Road will close at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks starting Monday, Nov. 30, weather permitting, for guardrail repairs, with a detour on Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73. Lane restrictions for ODOT's State Route 73 resurfacing run through Nov. 30. *([County Engineer](https://engineer.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Information/NewsReleases/2026/20261130_2_Week_Closure_of_Red_Lion_5_Points_Rd.pdf) · [OHGO](https://www.ohgo.com/))*
