## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Last day for the Sauerkraut Festival.** The 56th Ohio Sauerkraut Festival is open downtown today from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., per the Chamber. The National Weather Service calls for showers and thunderstorms likely, with a high of 69°, so bring rain gear. The Ohio Renaissance Festival's Time Travelers Weekend also wraps up today (10:30 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.), and the Devil's Staircase hillclimb near Oregonia runs its second day. *([Festival](https://sauerkrautfestival.waynesvilleohio.com/home/))*
2. **No school Monday; early voting resumes.** Wayne Local students are off Monday for fall break, per the district. Also Monday, early in-person voting for the November 3 general election resumes from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at the Warren County Board of Elections, 520 Justice Dr., Lebanon. *([Board of Elections schedule](https://vote.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Public_Election_Notice.pdf))*
3. **The week ahead.** Warren County Commissioners meet Tuesday at 9:00 a.m. in Lebanon. Girls Soccer Senior Night is Tuesday, when the varsity hosts Bellbrook at 7:15 p.m. Friday brings a two-hour early release to end the quarter, then Football Senior Night as the Spartans (fresh off a 45-7 win over Madison) host Eaton at 7:00 p.m. *([MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/eaton-vs-waynesville/10-16-2026/?c=ae4a8cf1-eaf5-4c11-8eeb-35412dd55d0b))*
