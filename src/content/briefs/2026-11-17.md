## This morning in Waynesville


1. **Alert test on TV and radio at 2:20 p.m.** FEMA and the FCC test the nationwide Emergency Alert System today between 2:20 and 2:50 p.m. on broadcast and cable TV and radio; it will not go to cellphones, and no action is needed. The backup date is Thursday, Dec. 3. *([FEMA](https://www.fema.gov/press-release/20260916/fema-and-fcc-schedule-2026-nationwide-test-emergency-alert-system-november))*
2. **Transportation survey closes tonight.** Today is the last day to comment on the North Warren County Transportation Plan, including I-75 to I-71 connection options, one of which would follow US 42 and S.R. 73 through Waynesville and Corwin. *([survey and plan](https://northwarrencountytransportationplan-burgessniple.hub.arcgis.com/pages/f27bc61005b842439ceb569b82c0b9cf))*
3. **Send the Spartans off Friday.** Waynesville Football invites the village to line the route at 4:30 p.m. Friday as the team leaves for the Region 20 final against Bishop Fenwick, 7 p.m. at Lakota East. Buses turn right out of the school, right on Main Street, then right on S.R. 73. *([flyer](/images/events/2026-11-20-spartan-send-off.webp))*
