## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Line the route Friday afternoon.** The All-Village Spartan Send-Off starts when the buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 p.m. Friday; fans are asked to line Dayton Road and North Street in orange and black. The Spartans, 11-2 and Region 20 champions, play Region 19 champion Ironton, 12-1, at 7 p.m. at Teays Valley High School in Ashville in the first state semifinal in Waynesville history. Tickets are $16 for adults and $10 for students online at ohsaa.org/tickets, the gate takes cash only, parking is $5, and only bags 5 by 8 by 1 inches or smaller are allowed. The National Weather Service forecast for Ashville calls for a chance of rain before 10 p.m. Friday, with a low around 33. Spectrum is streaming the game, and the winner plays for the state title Friday, Dec. 4. *([game-day details](/events/) · [Spartans Athletics](https://www.facebook.com/waynesvilleAth/posts/pfbid0CzTD3Kb4qvEQMRpVKLWLgyDw7rqjXSAtHA4HphMKJ5yQE9vr2S8bCjDRWGDG3NT9l) · [MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/ironton-vs-waynesville/11-27-2026/?c=ed4a7889-09b4-4b7e-8729-17891320fd62))*
2. **A fireball the night before Thanksgiving.** A bright meteor streaked across the sky around 7:40 p.m. Wednesday, and more than 1,300 people from Ohio, Indiana, Michigan and neighboring states reported it to the American Meteor Society. The society's estimate places it near the Detroit area, so from southwest Ohio it would have appeared low in the northern sky. If you saw it, you can file your own report with the society. *([our story](/stories/2026-11-26-fireball-meteor/) · [American Meteor Society](https://www.amsmeteors.org/fireballs/viewer/))*
3. **Red Lion-Five Points Road closes Monday.** The Warren County Engineer will close Red Lion-Five Points Road at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks starting Monday, Nov. 30, weather permitting, to repair the bridge guardrail. The detour uses Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73. *([County Engineer](https://engineer.warrencountyohio.gov/doc/Information/NewsReleases/2026/20261130_2_Week_Closure_of_Red_Lion_5_Points_Rd.pdf))*
