## This morning in Waynesville

1. **Wayne Local Schools earned an overall 4.5-star rating** on the 2025-26 Ohio School Report Cards, released by the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce. Waynesville High School and Waynesville Elementary each earned 5 stars; Waynesville Middle School earned 4 stars, up from 3.5 last year. ([district news](https://www.wayne-local.com/district-news/entry/wayne-local-schools-earns-4-5-rating-on-ohio-school-report-card/))
