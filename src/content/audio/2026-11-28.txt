Good morning, Waynesville. It's Saturday, November 28, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

It's a partly sunny start, with a high near 48 and a low tonight around 35. Rain showers move in Sunday, with a high near 53, and Monday turns partly sunny and colder, with a high near 42. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: the Spartans' historic season is over. Ironton beat Waynesville 35 to 10 Friday night in the Division Five state semifinal at Teays Valley High School in Ashville. It ends a season in which the Spartans reached the state final four for the first time in program history, after winning the Region 20 title with a 27 to nothing shutout of Bishop Fenwick. Spartans Athletics congratulated the team on a phenomenal season, and Wayne Local Schools said, we're so proud of you.

If you're heading out to see holiday lights, tonight looks like the night. Yuletide Village, at Renaissance Park on State Route 73, is open from 3 to 9 tonight, its Saturday hours through December 19, with tickets sold online. Today's partly sunny forecast makes it the better night of the weekend, with rain showers expected Sunday. Our Holiday Lights guide lists a dozen displays in and around Waynesville, with dates, hours and prices.

Heads up for Monday. The Warren County Engineer is closing Red Lion-Five Points Road at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks, weather permitting, to repair the guardrail. The detour uses Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73.

In schools, classes resume Monday after Thanksgiving break, and the Spartan Holiday Food Drive starts that day, collecting nonperishable food through December 11. The district holds a community levy listening session Monday, December 7, at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center lobby. And the girls basketball team plays at Clinton-Massie Monday at 6.

In local government, the Wayne Township trustees meet Tuesday at 7 p.m. The county commissioners' Tuesday agendas include a 60-day review of the temporary data center moratorium, a 2027 budget work session, and a waterline and sanitary sewer design agreement up for consideration.

On the roads, the Ohio State Highway Patrol is watching for impaired and distracted drivers through late Sunday night, the Warren County Post reports.

Around town, today is Small Business Saturday, a good day to shop and eat at Waynesville's locally owned businesses downtown and along Route 73. Hours vary by shop, so check before you go. And next weekend, December 4 through 6, Christmas in the Village fills downtown, with Santa at the Main Street gazebo and the Parade of Santas at 1 p.m. Sunday.
