Good morning, Waynesville. It's Monday, November 16, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

It's a mostly sunny start to the week, with a high near 54 today and a low around 33. Sunny skies carry through Tuesday and Wednesday, and it warms up to the low 60s by Thursday. The National Weather Service has no alerts in effect.

Our top story: the Spartans are one win from the regional title. After beating top-seeded Graham Local 14 to 7, Waynesville plays Bishop Fenwick in the Division Five, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. Friday. The game is at a neutral site, Lakota East High School in Liberty Township. Tickets are sold online through the Ohio High School Athletic Association. High school students can ride a spirit bus to the game; pick up a permission slip in the high school office and turn it in by Wednesday.

Next, if you want a say in the North Warren County Transportation Plan, Tuesday is your last day. The online survey asks about options for a new connection between Interstate 75 and Interstate 71, including one that would follow U.S. 42 and State Route 73 through Waynesville and Corwin, and about busy intersections like State Route 73 and U.S. 42 here in town.

And Village Council meets at 7 tonight at the Municipal Building on Lytle Road. On the agenda is a plan to split the cost of a retaining wall at the Hammel House on South Main Street, with the village paying half, up to 30 thousand dollars. Council will also consider appointing a new law director and naming a stretch of right-of-way between Third and Main as Chapman Street.

On Tuesday, the Warren County commissioners will consider a quote for a geotechnical study for improvements at the Waynesville wastewater treatment plant, and they hold a work session on the 2027 county budget.

In schools, wrestling families have parent meetings in the middle and high school cafeteria: seventh and eighth grade at 4:20 this afternoon, and high school at 6 p.m. Tuesday. The board of education next meets December 14.

A heads up: the nationwide Emergency Alert System test runs on TV and radio Tuesday afternoon between 2:20 and 2:50. It won't go to cellphones, and you don't need to do anything. And expect lane restrictions on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through the end of the month.

Looking ahead, holiday light season starts this week, and downtown Waynesville becomes Stars Hollow for Town Square Days, Saturday and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., rain or shine.
