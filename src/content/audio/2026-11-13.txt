Good morning, Waynesville. It's Friday, November 13, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect a chance of light rain today with a high of 46 and a low of 29. Saturday looks sunny with a high of 47, and Sunday turns mostly cloudy with a high of 48. There are no National Weather Service alerts in effect.

Our top story: it's playoff night. The Spartans, who beat Preble Shawnee 35 to 13 last week, travel to St. Paris tonight to play Graham Local in the Division Five, Region 20 semifinal. Kickoff is at 7 p.m. at Graham High School. The team's game-day flyer says to buy tickets online through the state athletic association's Hometown Ticketing, because the gate takes cash only. If you can't make the drive, Graham streams the game on HUDL for a fee.

Before that, the whole village is invited to send the team off. The buses leave Waynesville High School at 4 p.m., turn left out of the school and head out of town on Lytle Road. Wear orange and black, and line Dayton and Lytle roads to cheer them on.

And Saturday is a big day in town. The free Waynesville Fantasy Festival fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Owl Fest runs at Caesar Creek State Park from 3 to 11 p.m.

In the schools, the district celebrated 12 Spartan Recognition Award honorees before Monday's Board of Education meeting. Today is the last day of the Waynesville Elementary book fair, and basketball scrimmages start Saturday. No delays or closings are posted.

Also this weekend, you can try lacrosse with the Waynesville Spartans Lacrosse club Saturday from 1 to 3 p.m. at Spartan Community Field. Saturday evening, CrossView Christian Church on Social Row Road hosts a dessert auction from 6 to 8 p.m., with proceeds going to the Miami Cemetery Association Roads Fund. Over in Lebanon, The City Mercantile Christmas Market runs all weekend at the Warren County Fairgrounds. And FOX19 reports that Cincinnati's Night of Lights is moving to those same fairgrounds this year, with more than two million lights set to holiday music.

In local government, the Wayne Township Trustees meet Tuesday at 7 p.m. That same day, the online survey for the North Warren County Transportation Plan closes, so if you have thoughts on the area's roads, now is the time to share them.

On the roads, lane restrictions continue on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through November 30 for resurfacing and a new turn lane.
