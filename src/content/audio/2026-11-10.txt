Good morning, Waynesville. It's Tuesday, November 10, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect sunshine today and a high near 67, with a low around 41 tonight. Veterans Day looks partly sunny with a high of 63. A chance of light rain arrives Thursday, and Friday turns colder, with a high near 44. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: the county's road planners come to town tonight. Warren County Engineer Kurt Weber will talk about the North Warren County Transportation Plan at 7 p.m. at the Village of Waynesville office on Lytle Road. The Chamber asks people who plan to attend to RSVP. Local 12 reports that planners have drawn six proposals for a better connection between Interstate 71 and Interstate 75 across the northern part of the county, and that any construction is years away. The plan's online survey closes November 17.

Next, Wayne Local Schools invites veterans and active-duty military members to a special morning on Veterans Day. There's a breakfast from 7:45 to 8:30 a.m., a program from 8:50 to 9:30, and a veterans car parade from 9:30 to 10. The district asks guests to RSVP. Wayne Township's administrative offices will be closed Wednesday for the holiday.

And in sports, the Spartans are still rolling. Fresh off a 35 to 13 playoff win over Preble Shawnee, Waynesville plays a regional semifinal at Graham Local in Saint Paris on Friday at 7 p.m.

In the schools, high school students recently gathered for another Supper Club, a meal that pairs students with community members and staff to talk about careers and practice networking. The Waynesville Elementary Book Fair runs through Friday, with a family night Thursday from 4 to 7 p.m.

In local government, the Warren County commissioners meet at 9 this morning. Up for consideration are contracts for an apron and taxiway project at the county airport, spending opioid settlement money for the county drug task force, and starting talks to replace the emergency communications center's recording system. A 2027 budget work session follows at 11:30. The Wayne Township trustees meet next Tuesday at 7 p.m.

Looking ahead to Saturday, the Waynesville Fantasy Festival fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Caesar Creek State Park hosts Owl Fest from 3 to 11 p.m.
