Good morning, Waynesville. It's Sunday, November 22nd, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. It's another mild one, mostly sunny with a high near 69 and a low around 40 tonight. Enjoy it, because the week turns colder fast. Monday is sunny with a high of 50 and a low near 28, Tuesday tops out around 45, and Wednesday brings clouds and a slight chance of light rain. There are no weather alerts this morning.

Our top story: the Spartans are headed to the first state semifinal in school history. After Friday night's 27 to nothing win over Bishop Fenwick for the regional title, Waynesville, now 11 and 2, plays Ironton, the Region 19 champion at 12 and 1, in a Division Five state semifinal. Kickoff is 7 p.m. this Friday, November 27th, at Teays Valley High School in Ashville. The winner plays for the state championship on December 4th. The state athletic association says playoff tickets go on sale on Tuesdays on its website.

Second, it's the last day in Stars Hollow. Town Square Days wraps up downtown today, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m., and the weather should cooperate.

Third, it's Thanksgiving week. Wayne Local Schools are closed Monday through Friday for the break. And if your family could use a little help this year, MorningStar Baptist Church's outreach ministry is still taking requests for free Thanksgiving food baskets for Waynesville families. You can find how to reach them in today's written brief.

In schools, when students return, the Spartan Holiday Food Drive runs November 30th through December 11th. The district's Community Levy Listening Session is set for 6 p.m. Monday, December 7th, in the Performing Arts Center lobby, ahead of the school board's December 14th meeting. And at Mary L. Cook Public Library, registration for a December 7th kids' art class, making Shrinky Dink keychains, opens Monday.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet Tuesday at 9 a.m. in Lebanon. Their agenda had not been posted as of this morning. The Wayne Township trustees meet next on December 1st at 7 p.m.

Around town, holiday light season arrives Friday, when Yuletide Village opens its Season of Lights on Friday and Saturday nights through December 19th, and Kings Island's WinterFest opens in Mason the same night. Christmas in the Village follows downtown December 4th through the 6th.

And on the roads, the State Route 73 resurfacing between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road keeps lane restrictions in place through November 30th, so allow a little extra time for holiday travel.
