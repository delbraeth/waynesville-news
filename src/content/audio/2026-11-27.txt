Good morning, Waynesville. It's Friday, November 27, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

It's a rainy start, with a high near 44 and gusty winds. Rain is likely today, with a chance of showers before 10 tonight and a low around 31. Saturday turns mostly sunny, with a high near 49, and light rain returns Sunday. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: it's time to send off the Spartans. The All-Village Spartan Send-Off starts when the buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 this afternoon. The route turns right out of school, right on U.S. 42 and left on State Route 73, and fans are asked to line it in orange and black. Waynesville, eleven and two and the Region 20 champion, plays Region 19 champion Ironton at 7 p.m. at Teays Valley High School in Ashville, in the first state semifinal in Waynesville history. Tickets are 16 dollars for adults and 10 for students online, the gate takes cash only, parking is 5 dollars, and only small bags are allowed. In Ashville tonight, expect a chance of rain before midnight, a low around 32 and gusty winds. Can't make the trip? Spectrum is streaming the game, and the winner plays for the state title on Friday, December 4.

Holiday lights season opens tonight. Yuletide Village opens at Renaissance Park on State Route 73, Fridays from 5 to 9 and Saturdays from 3 to 9, through December 19, with tickets sold online and a Black Friday sale today. WinterFest at Kings Island, the Clifton Mill light display and Hillsboro's Winter Wonderland also open tonight. Our Holiday Lights guide lists a dozen displays in and around Waynesville, with dates, hours and prices.

Heads up for Monday. The Warren County Engineer is closing Red Lion-Five Points Road at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks, weather permitting, to repair the guardrail. The detour uses Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73.

In schools, Wayne Local is on Thanksgiving break today. When classes resume Monday, the Spartan Holiday Food Drive begins, collecting nonperishable food through December 11. And the football team got a hand from the University of Dayton, which let the Spartans practice in its indoor facility Thursday morning.

In local government, Wayne Township offices are closed today, and the trustees meet Tuesday at 7 p.m. The county commissioners' Tuesday agendas include a 60-day review of the temporary data center moratorium, a 2027 budget work session, and a waterline and sanitary sewer design agreement up for consideration.

On the roads, the Ohio State Highway Patrol is watching for impaired and distracted drivers through late Sunday night, the Warren County Post reports.

Looking ahead, Christmas in the Village fills downtown Waynesville next weekend, December 4 through 6, with Santa at the Main Street gazebo and the Parade of Santas at 1 p.m. Sunday.
