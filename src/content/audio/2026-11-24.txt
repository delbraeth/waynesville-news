Good morning, Waynesville. It's Tuesday, November 24, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. It's a sunny, cold day, with a high near 44 and a low near 27 tonight. Wednesday turns partly sunny and a bit milder. The National Weather Service is calling for rain on Thanksgiving Day, with a high near 48, and rain and snow on Friday. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Now, the big story. Waynesville is getting ready to send its football team off to the first state semifinal in school history. Spartans Athletics is calling for an All-Village Send-Off on Black Friday. The buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 in the afternoon, turn right out of school, right on U.S. 42, and left on State Route 73. Line the route and wear orange and black. The Spartans, at 11 and 2, play Ironton, at 12 and 1, at 7 p.m. Friday at Teays Valley High School in Ashville. The Ohio High School Athletic Association says playoff tickets go on sale on Tuesdays through its website.

Drivers, take note. The Warren County Engineer says Red Lion-Five Points Road will close at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks starting Monday, November 30, for guardrail repairs. The detour uses Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73. Lane restrictions for the State Route 73 resurfacing project continue through November 30.

In schools, Wayne Local is on Thanksgiving break, with no classes through Friday. The district gave a shout-out to the Waynesville Marching Band for its season of Friday night shows. When classes resume, the Spartan Holiday Food Drive runs through December 11, and the district holds a community levy listening session on Monday, December 7, at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center lobby.

In local government, the county commissioners have no session this week. On December 1, they'll take up bids for a county bridge project, water and sewer design work, a review of the temporary data center moratorium, and their 2027 budget. Wayne Township offices are closed Thursday and Friday for Thanksgiving.

Looking ahead, Christmas in the Village fills downtown December 4 through 6, with luminaries, Santa at the Gazebo, and a Christmas Market street faire with a Parade of Santas on Sunday afternoon. Farther afield, WinterFest opens at Kings Island in Mason on Friday. And if you're shopping for a good cause, the Friends of the Library holiday auction runs at the Mary L. Cook Public Library through December 10.
