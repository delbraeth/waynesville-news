Good morning, Waynesville. It's Thursday, November 12, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect a chance of light rain today with a high of 54 and a low of 36. Friday turns colder, with a high of 45 and a low of 28. The weekend looks mostly sunny, with highs in the mid to upper 40s and lows near 30. There are no National Weather Service alerts in effect.

Our top story this morning: it's family night at the Waynesville Elementary book fair. Families can stop by the school from 4 to 7 p.m. today, and the fair wraps up Friday. Then at 6 p.m., the Mary L. Cook Public Library holds its Christmas Book Flood pajama storytime, and children go home with a book.

Next, the Spartans are on the road Friday for a Division Five regional semifinal at Graham High School in St. Paris. Waynesville Football is asking the whole village to wear orange and black and line Lytle Road as the team buses leave the high school at 4 p.m. Kickoff is at 7 p.m., and it will be cold, with an overnight low near 28. Waynesville comes in at nine and two after beating Preble Shawnee 35 to 13.

And Saturday is a big day close to home. The free Waynesville Fantasy Festival fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. Over at Caesar Creek State Park, Owl Fest runs from 3 to 11 p.m., starting at the Visitor Center, and the park says the Ohio School of Falconry will be there this weekend starting at 6 p.m.

In schools, Wayne Local shared more photos and thanks from Wednesday's Veterans Day events. Basketball scrimmages begin Saturday. No delays or closings are posted.

Also Saturday, Spartans Lacrosse hosts a free try lacrosse event for boys and girls from 1 to 3 p.m. at Spartan Community Field, with gear provided. And CrossView Christian Church holds a dessert auction from 6 to 8 p.m. to benefit the Miami Cemetery Association Roads Fund. In Lebanon, The City Mercantile Christmas Market runs Friday through Sunday at the Warren County Fairgrounds.

In local government, Wayne Township trustees meet Tuesday at 7 p.m. Tuesday is also the last day to take the online survey for the North Warren County Transportation Plan. The county commissioners meet Tuesday morning in Lebanon.

On the roads, lane restrictions continue on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through November 30, and the Morrow Woodville Road closure is set to run through Friday.
