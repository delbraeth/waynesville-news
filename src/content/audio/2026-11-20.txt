Good morning, Waynesville. It's Friday, November 20, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect a sunny day with a high of 68 after a morning low near 40. Saturday looks just as nice, sunny with a high of 68. Sunday stays mild, around 66, with a slight chance of light rain later. Then it turns cooler Monday, with a high of 50. There are no weather alerts this morning.

Our top story: the Spartans play for the region title tonight. Waynesville meets Bishop Fenwick in the Division Five, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. at Lakota East High School in Liberty Township. Before that, the whole village is invited to the send-off at 4:30 p.m. at the high school. The buses will head out onto Main Street and then State Route 73, so line the route and cheer them on. The athletic department says Spartans towels will be available at the game.

Second, it's Stars Hollow weekend downtown. Town Square Days runs from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday and from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Sunday, rain or shine. Main Street will be closed between North and Miami streets. For the shuttle, park at the Waynesville Performing Arts Center on Dayton Road. On Saturday late morning, Turn Out Dance Academy is holding a dance marathon, and entry is two canned goods or a five-dollar donation for the Waynesville Food Pantry.

Third, the Friends of the Library holiday auction opens at noon today at the Mary L. Cook Public Library. You can bid in person on donated gift baskets and decorations until 7 p.m. on December 10th, and the money supports the library's free programs. The Letters to Santa mailbox is up at the library too.

In schools, sophomores at Waynesville High School visited the Warren County Career Center on Thursday for its Sophomore Explore event and toured the career-tech labs. The Food Services team also served students and staff a special Thanksgiving meal this week. Boys basketball scrimmages Roger Bacon Monday at 6 p.m., then plays Kenton Ridge on Tuesday.

In local government, the village says Sunday's water main breaks on Mill Street and Laura Marie Drive have both been fixed. The Warren County commissioners meet again Tuesday morning in Lebanon.

Around the area, the Nights of Lights drive-through show opens tonight at the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon. And the Cincinnati Zoo announced that Fiona, its beloved Nile hippo, has died. She would have turned 10 in January.

One reminder: it's youth deer gun season this weekend, and Caesar Creek State Park reminds visitors that hunting is allowed in some areas near its trails. Lane restrictions continue on State Route 73 through the end of the month.
