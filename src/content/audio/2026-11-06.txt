Good morning, Waynesville. It's Friday, November 6, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect a partly sunny day with a high of 55. Frost is possible tonight, with a low near 30, and the weekend stays cool and mostly sunny, with frost each morning. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: playoff football is back at Spartan Community Field tonight. Waynesville hosts Preble Shawnee in the second round at 7 p.m. If you're going, bring a warm layer.

Also today, Hearthwarming Holidays opens downtown. The holiday shopping weekend runs through Sunday, with shops open from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. today and Saturday, and from noon to 5 p.m. on Sunday. Nearby, the CrossView Christmas Bazaar at CrossView Christian Church on East Social Row Road runs today from 3 to 7 p.m. and Saturday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

And the Dayton Daily News reports that a new study proposes six connectors between Interstate 75 and Interstate 71 in northern Warren County. It's part of the North Warren County Transportation Plan. The county engineer will present the plan at the village office on Lytle Road this Tuesday at 7 p.m., and the plan's online survey is open through November 17.

In schools, Wayne Local invites the community to send off the boys cross country team Saturday at 11:20 a.m. The district also says 30 Waynesville High School freshmen and sophomores recently volunteered during a community service visit. The Board of Education meets Monday at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center, with Spartan Recognition Awards.

At the library on Saturday, there's a Paint Party at 10 a.m. and a Gingerbread workshop at 11.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet Tuesday morning, though they haven't posted that agenda yet. The Wayne Township Trustees meet November 17.

On the roads, the State Route 73 resurfacing and turn lane project between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road continues, with lane restrictions through November 30, according to the Ohio Department of Transportation.

Looking ahead, the Waynesville Fantasy Festival comes downtown Saturday, November 14, the same day as Owl Fest at Caesar Creek State Park. And the City Mercantile Christmas Market fills the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon from November 13 through 15.

One more note: MorningStar Baptist Church's outreach ministry is taking requests for free Thanksgiving food baskets for Waynesville families ahead of the holiday.
