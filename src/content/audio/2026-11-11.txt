Good morning, Waynesville. It's Wednesday, November 11, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. On this Veterans Day, expect mostly sunny skies and a high near 65, with a low around 42 tonight. A chance of light rain arrives Thursday with a high of 51, and it turns colder from there. Friday looks partly sunny with a high of 45 and a low near 28, and Saturday should be sunny with a high of 46. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: Wayne Local Schools is saluting veterans this morning. The district invited veterans and active-duty military members to a breakfast from 7:45 to 8:30, a program from 8:50 to 9:30, and a veterans car parade from 9:30 to 10. Wayne Township's administrative offices are closed today for the holiday.

Next, Spartan football. Waynesville, now nine and two, travels to Graham High School in St. Paris on Friday for a regional semifinal. Kickoff is at 7 p.m. The Spartans beat Preble Shawnee 35 to 13 last week. Bundle up, because temperatures will fall into the upper 20s Friday night, and the state athletic association sells playoff tickets online.

Saturday is a full day close to home. The free Waynesville Fantasy Festival runs downtown from 11 in the morning to 8 at night. Caesar Creek State Park hosts Owl Fest from 3 to 11 p.m., starting at the Visitor Center. And CrossView Christian Church holds a dessert auction from 6 to 8 p.m. to benefit the Miami Cemetery Association Roads Fund. A little farther away, The City Mercantile Christmas Market fills the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon from Friday through Sunday. The organizer lists admission at 12 dollars Friday, 10 dollars Saturday and 5 dollars Sunday, and children 12 and under get in free.

In the schools, the Waynesville Elementary Book Fair wraps up Friday, with a family night Thursday from 4 to 7 p.m. Also Thursday, at 6 p.m., the Mary L. Cook Public Library holds its Christmas Book Flood pajama storytime, and children go home with a book and a cocoa packet. No registration is needed. No school delays or closings are posted.

In local government, the Wayne Township trustees meet next Tuesday at 7 p.m. That same day is the last day to take the online survey for the North Warren County Transportation Plan.

On the roads, lane restrictions continue on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through the end of the month. And Caesar Creek State Park has winterized for the season, so flush toilets are no longer available there.
