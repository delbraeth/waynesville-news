Good morning, Waynesville. It's Saturday, November 7, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Patchy frost early gives way to a mostly cloudy day, with a high of 54. Tonight drops to about 35, and Sunday and Monday mornings bring widespread frost before sunny afternoons. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: the Spartans are moving on. Waynesville beat Preble Shawnee 35 to 13 last night at Spartan Community Field. Next up is a regional semifinal on the road at Graham Local in St. Paris, Friday at 7 p.m.

Another Spartan team is chasing a title today. The boys cross country team runs at the state meet, and the district invited the community to send the team off at 11:20 this morning.

And downtown, Hearthwarming Holidays continues. Shops are open from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. today and from noon to 5 p.m. Sunday. The CrossView Christmas Bazaar at CrossView Christian Church on East Social Row Road wraps up today, from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

In schools, the Wayne Local Board of Education meets Monday at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center, with Spartan Recognition Awards. No delays or closings are posted.

Around town, American Legion Post 615 holds its annual Veterans Day dinner Sunday starting at 3 p.m. Next Saturday, the Waynesville Fantasy Festival comes downtown, the same day as Owl Fest at Caesar Creek State Park. At the Mary L. Cook Public Library, today brings a paint party at 10 a.m. and the Gingerbread 101 Workshop from 11 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. And a little farther afield, Kings Island is open for a bonus weekend today and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m., and the City Mercantile Christmas Market fills the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon next weekend, November 13 through 15.

In local government, the Warren County commissioners meet Tuesday morning. Up for consideration are contracts for an apron and taxiway project at the county airport, spending opioid settlement money for the county drug task force, and negotiations to replace the recording system at the county's emergency communications center. A 2027 budget work session follows. Then Tuesday at 7 p.m., the county engineer presents the North Warren County Transportation Plan at the village office on Lytle Road. The plan's online survey is open through November 17.

On the roads, lane restrictions continue on State Route 73 between Red Lion-Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through the end of November.
