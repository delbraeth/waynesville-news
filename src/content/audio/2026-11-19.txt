Good morning, Waynesville. It's Thursday, November 19, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. It's a sunny one today, with a high of 63 and a low tonight of 41. Friday and Saturday stay sunny and get even milder, with highs of 67 and 68. Sunday turns partly sunny, with a chance of light rain later. There are no weather alerts in effect this morning.

Now, the top stories.

The Spartans play for a regional title tomorrow night. Waynesville meets Bishop Fenwick in the Division Five, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. Friday at Lakota East High School in Liberty Township. Before that, the whole village is invited to the send-off at 4:30 p.m. outside the high school. The buses head down Main Street and out State Route 73, so line the route and cheer them on. The Spartans reached the final by beating Graham Local 14 to 7 last Friday.

This weekend, downtown Waynesville becomes Stars Hollow. Town Square Days runs from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday and Sunday, rain or shine, presented by the Waynesville Merchant Association.

And Nights of Lights opens tomorrow at the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon. The drive-through show runs nightly through January 2 by timed ticket. It's 30 dollars for a carload of up to eight, and tickets are sold online. Our Holiday Lights guide has more displays nearby.

In schools, the Waynesville Elementary PTO meets at 7 p.m. tonight at the elementary school. Boys basketball scrimmages Roger Bacon at 6 p.m. Monday, then faces Kenton Ridge on Tuesday. At the Mary L. Cook Public Library, the Letters to Santa mailbox opens tomorrow, and letters dropped off by December 15 get a reply. The Friends of the Library Holiday Auction also opens at noon tomorrow.

Around town, the Chamber's Business to Business networking meets at 5 p.m. today at A Chick with Wicks. And MorningStar Baptist Church is taking requests for free Thanksgiving food baskets for Waynesville families.

In local government, the county commissioners next meet Tuesday at 9 a.m. in Lebanon. Their agenda isn't posted yet.

A safety note: youth deer gun season is this weekend, and Caesar Creek State Park reminds visitors that hunting is allowed in areas near its trails. Lane restrictions continue on State Route 73 between Red Lion-Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through November 30.
