Good morning, Waynesville. It's Wednesday, November 25, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

It's a mostly sunny start, with a high near 50 today. Thanksgiving Day brings light rain and a high near 47, and more light rain is expected Friday. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: Wayne Local Schools wants to hear from you about its next levy. The district will hold a Community Levy Listening Session on Monday, December 7, from 6 to 7:30 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center lobby. The district will share its financial outlook and ask two questions about 2027: whether to go to voters in May or November, and whether to seek a property tax or an earned income tax. Board President Amanda Garrett will lead the discussion, with Treasurer Carolyn Huber and Superintendent Sam Ison answering questions. The feedback will help shape the board's discussion at its December 14 meeting.

If you're on the road for the holiday, the Ohio State Highway Patrol is focusing on impaired and distracted drivers from midnight tonight through late Sunday night, the Warren County Post reports.

And if you're heading to Friday's state semifinal, Wayne Local and Spartans Athletics have posted game-day information for fans, and they ask everyone attending to read it first. Waynesville plays Ironton at 7 p.m. at Teays Valley High School. The All-Village Send-Off starts when the buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 p.m.

In schools, Wayne Local is on Thanksgiving break through Friday. When students return Monday, the Student Council's Spartan Holiday Food Drive with American Legion Post 615 begins, and it runs through December 11. The Mary L. Cook Public Library is closed Thursday for Thanksgiving.

In local government, Wayne Township offices are closed Thursday and Friday and reopen Monday, when the trustees meet at 7 p.m. The county commissioners' December 1 agendas include a 60-day review of the temporary data center moratorium and a 2027 budget work session.

On the roads, Red Lion-Five Points Road closes at a bridge south of Crossley Road for about two weeks starting Monday, with a detour on Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73.

Looking ahead, Christmas in the Village returns to downtown Waynesville December 4 through 6, with Santa at the Main Street gazebo and the Parade of Santas at 1 p.m. Sunday.

Happy Thanksgiving. That's your brief. Read the full edition, with links to every source, at waynesville dot news.
