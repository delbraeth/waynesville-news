Good morning, Waynesville. It's Tuesday, November 17, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect some patchy fog early, then sunshine, with a high near 56. Tonight drops to about 34. The rest of the week stays sunny and mild, with highs from 62 Wednesday to 67 on Friday. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Now, three things to know this morning.

If your TV or radio sounds an alert this afternoon, don't worry. FEMA and the Federal Communications Commission are testing the nationwide Emergency Alert System between 2:20 and 2:50 p.m. It runs on broadcast and cable TV and radio only. It will not go to cellphones, and no action is needed. If it gets postponed, the backup date is Thursday, December 3.

Second, today is the last day to weigh in on the North Warren County Transportation Plan. The online survey asks about options for a new connection between Interstate 75 and Interstate 71, including one that would follow U.S. 42 and State Route 73 through Waynesville and Corwin. You'll find the link at waynesville dot news.

Third, get ready to cheer. Waynesville Football is inviting the whole village to line the route at 4:30 p.m. Friday as the Spartans leave for the Division Five, Region 20 final against Bishop Fenwick. Kickoff is at 7 p.m. at Lakota East High School in Liberty Township. The buses turn right out of the school, right on Main Street, then right on State Route 73.

In schools, high school wrestling families meet at 6 tonight in the middle and high school cafeteria. The district has posted its latest board meeting recap, and the board of education next meets December 14 at 6 p.m.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet this morning. On the agenda is a quote for geotechnical work on improvement projects at the Lower Little Miami and Waynesville wastewater treatment plants, along with a public hearing on county subdivision rules and a 2027 budget work session. The Wayne Township Trustees meet at 7 tonight at the township building on North Clarksville Road.

The village also reports that weekend water main breaks on Mill Street and Laura Marie Drive have been fixed.

And looking ahead to the weekend, downtown Waynesville turns into Stars Hollow for Town Square Days, Saturday and Sunday from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m., rain or shine.

That's your brief. Read the full edition, with links to every source, at waynesville dot news.
