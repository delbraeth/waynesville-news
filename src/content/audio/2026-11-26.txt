Good morning, Waynesville. It's Thursday, November 26, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News. Happy Thanksgiving.

It's a rainy Thanksgiving, with a high near 47 and a low around 35 tonight. The National Weather Service says three quarters of an inch to an inch of rain is possible today, with gusty winds. Friday brings rain early, then rain and snow are likely, with a high near 45. Saturday turns mostly sunny, and there are no weather alerts in effect.

Our top story: it's time to send off the Spartans. The All-Village Spartan Send-Off starts when the buses leave Waynesville High School at 3:45 Friday afternoon, and fans are asked to line Dayton Road and North Street in orange and black. Waynesville, eleven and two and the Region 20 champion, plays Region 19 champion Ironton at 7 p.m. at Teays Valley High School in Ashville. It's the first state semifinal in Waynesville history. Tickets are 16 dollars for adults and 10 for students online, the gate takes cash only, parking is 5 dollars, and only small bags are allowed. In Ashville, the forecast calls for a chance of rain before 10 p.m. Can't make the trip? Spectrum is streaming the game, and the winner plays for the state title on Friday, December 4.

Did you see the sky light up last night? A bright fireball streaked across the sky around 7:40 Wednesday evening, and more than 1,300 people across Ohio, Indiana, Michigan and neighboring states reported it to the American Meteor Society. The society places it near Detroit, so from here it would have appeared low in the northern sky. If you saw it, you can add your own report.

Heads up for Monday. The Warren County Engineer is closing Red Lion-Five Points Road at a bridge just south of Crossley Road for about two weeks, weather permitting, to repair the guardrail. The detour uses Bunnell Hill Road and State Route 73.

In schools, Wayne Local is on Thanksgiving break through Friday. When classes resume Monday, the Spartan Holiday Food Drive begins, running through December 11. And the district's levy listening session is Monday, December 7, at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center lobby.

In local government, Wayne Township offices are closed today and Friday, and the trustees meet Tuesday at 7 p.m. The county commissioners' Tuesday agendas include a 60-day review of the temporary data center moratorium a 2027 budget work session, and a waterline and sanitary sewer design agreement up for consideration.

On the roads, the Ohio State Highway Patrol is watching for impaired and distracted drivers through late Sunday night, the Warren County Post reports.

Looking ahead, Christmas in the Village fills downtown Waynesville next weekend, December 4 through 6, with more than a thousand luminaries, Santa at the Main Street gazebo, and the Parade of Santas at 1 p.m. Sunday. And WinterFest opens at Kings Island on Friday night.
