Good morning, Waynesville. It's Wednesday, November 4, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect rain today, with a high of 67 and a low near 50 tonight. Thursday turns mostly sunny with a high of 64. Friday brings a chance of light rain and a high of 59, and Saturday will be mostly sunny but chilly, with a low right at freezing. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Now, the top stories.

Voters renewed both county levies on Tuesday's ballot. According to unofficial results reported by the Warren County Post, the Warren County Senior Services renewal passed with about 68 percent of the vote, and the Mental Health Recovery Board renewal passed with about 64 percent. Statewide, the amendment requiring photo identification for in-person voting passed by a two-to-one margin, the Statehouse News Bureau reports. And in the Seventh Ohio Senate District, which covers all of Warren County, Republican Zac Haines defeated Democrat Cara Jacob in unofficial totals. He succeeds Senator Steve Wilson, who was term-limited. These results stay unofficial until they are certified.

Second, the Spartans host Preble Shawnee in varsity football this Friday at 7 p.m. And the Wayne Local Board of Education meets next Monday, November 9, at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center, with Spartan Recognition Awards.

Third, Hearthwarming Holidays opens downtown this Friday. The holiday shopping weekend runs Friday through Sunday, so check the Merchants Association event page for each day's hours.

In the schools, Young Fives students celebrated fall with pumpkin painting, carving and exploring the seeds inside. And the boys cross country team, third at regionals, is headed to state.

Looking ahead, the library hosts a Paint Party Saturday at 10 a.m. and a Gingerbread workshop at 11, both at the Mary L. Cook Public Library on Old Stage Road. The Waynesville Fantasy Festival comes downtown November 14, the same day as Owl Fest at Caesar Creek State Park, which runs from 3 to 11 p.m. And in Lebanon, the City Mercantile Christmas Market fills the county fairgrounds November 13 through 15.

On the roads, expect lane restrictions on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through November 30. And the North Warren County Transportation Plan survey closes November 17. The county commissioners meet next Tuesday at 9 a.m. in Lebanon.
