Good morning, Waynesville. It's Monday, November 23, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. It's a sunny, chilly start to Thanksgiving week, with a high near 49 today and a low near 27 tonight. Tuesday stays sunny and a little colder, with a high of 45. Wednesday turns partly sunny and milder, and the National Weather Service is calling for light rain on Thanksgiving Day, with a high near 48. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Now, the big story. The Spartans are in a state semifinal for the first time in school history. Waynesville, now 11 and 2, plays Region 19 champion Ironton, at 12 and 1, in a Division Five state semifinal at 7 p.m. Friday at Teays Valley High School in Ashville. The Ohio High School Athletic Association says playoff tickets go on sale on Tuesdays through its website. The winner plays for the state title on December 4.

Second, holiday light season opens this week. A Carillon Christmas in Dayton opens Tuesday, and Light Up Middletown starts on Thanksgiving night. On Friday, Yuletide Village's Season of Lights opens at Renaissance Park on State Route 73, along with WinterFest at Kings Island and the light display at Clifton Mill. The Christmas Ranch on Waynesville Road is already open, and it closes on Thanksgiving Day. Hillsboro's Winter Wonderland now has its 25 dollar per vehicle passes on sale. Our Holiday Lights guide has times and tickets for all of them.

In schools, Wayne Local is on Thanksgiving break all week, with no school through Friday. The football team's long run is reshaping the winter calendar. Spartans Athletics says it has changed the basketball schedule, and this week's varsity boys basketball scrimmage with Roger Bacon and Tuesday's game with Kenton Ridge are now listed as canceled. When classes resume, the Spartan Holiday Food Drive runs from November 30 through December 11.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet Tuesday at 9 a.m. in Lebanon. Their agenda had not been posted as of this morning. The Wayne Township trustees meet next on December 1 at 7 p.m.

On the roads, the State Route 73 resurfacing project between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road keeps lane restrictions in place through November 30, so allow extra time for Thanksgiving travel.

And looking ahead downtown, Christmas in the Village runs December 4 through 6, with Santa at the gazebo on Main Street and a Parade of Santas on Sunday afternoon.
