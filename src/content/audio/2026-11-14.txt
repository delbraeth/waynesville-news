Good morning, Waynesville. It's Saturday, November 14, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

It's a partly sunny day, with a high of 47 and a low of 34 tonight. Sunday looks partly sunny with a high of 49, and Monday turns sunny and a little milder, near 54. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Today belongs to the Waynesville Fantasy Festival. The free festival fills downtown from 11 in the morning until 8 tonight, with archery, falcon encounters, fire performers, live music on High Street and six food trucks. In the Dwarven Beer Garden, Sean Nos plays at 11 and again at 5, and Creep It Real plays at 12:30 and 3:30. Riddles in the Dark is at 3 at the Stone House Tavern, and the 5 o'clock bourbon tasting inside the Villager Pub requires preregistration.

On to the regional final. The Spartans beat Graham 14 to 7 last night in St. Paris in the Division Five, Region 20 semifinal. Next up is Bishop Fenwick, Friday, November 20, at 7 p.m., at a neutral site that has not been announced yet.

And the Wayne Local school board has started talking about a levy. At Monday's meeting, according to the district's board recap, members discussed whether to put an issue on the May or the November ballot, and Board President Amanda Garrett suggested an exploratory committee that would include staff. A May issue would need its groundwork done by February. Treasurer Carolyn Huber reported that revenue is running ahead of forecast, but the district's projected year-end cash balance still falls from 8.4 million dollars to 7.1 million next year.

In other school news, the board recognized eight students and four staff members with Spartan Recognition Awards. The high school's production of Radium Girls plays tonight at 7 and Sunday at 2 at the Performing Arts Center. The board meets next on December 14 at 6 p.m.

On Tuesday, the Warren County Commissioners will consider a quote for geotechnical work on improvements to the Waynesville wastewater treatment plant, and they'll hold a budget work session for 2027. Village Council meets Monday.

Also today, Owl Fest runs from 3 to 11 tonight at Caesar Creek State Park, and the Christmas market at the county fairgrounds in Lebanon continues through Sunday. Drivers, expect lane restrictions on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through the end of the month.
