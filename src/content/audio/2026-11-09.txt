Good morning, Waynesville. It's Monday, November 9, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect widespread frost early, then sunshine and a high near 62. Tuesday looks sunny and a little warmer, around 66. Veterans Day on Wednesday is partly sunny with a high near 64, and light rain is likely Thursday. There are no weather alerts this morning.

Our top story: the Wayne Local Board of Education meets tonight at 6 p.m. in the auditorium of the Performing Arts Center on Dayton Road. Spartan Recognition Awards are on the program.

Second, the North Warren County Transportation Plan comes to the village on Tuesday. Warren County Engineer Kurt Weber will talk about the plan at 7 p.m. at the Village of Waynesville office on Lytle Road, and the Chamber asks that you RSVP. Local 12 reports that planners have drawn six proposals for a better connection between Interstate 71 and Interstate 75 across the northern part of the county. One of them would follow State Route 122 east to U.S. 42 and then State Route 73. Any construction is years away. The plan's online survey closes November 17.

Third, a big week for the Spartans. After a 35 to 13 playoff win over Preble Shawnee, the football team plays a regional semifinal at Graham Local in St. Paris on Friday at 7 p.m. And on Saturday, the Waynesville Fantasy Festival fills downtown from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m.

In school news, Wayne Local is encouraging families to nominate teachers in a local law firm's educator recognition effort. And students in two classes recently built airplanes alongside Lowe's staff on a hands-on field trip. No delays or closings are posted.

Around town on Saturday, Owl Fest runs from 3 to 11 p.m. at Caesar Creek State Park, and CrossView Christian Church holds a dessert auction for the Miami Cemetery Association roads fund. The library is collecting donations for the Friends of the Library holiday silent auction through Saturday.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet Tuesday at 9 a.m. Up for consideration are contracts for an apron and taxiway project at the county airport, spending opioid settlement money for the county drug task force, and replacing the recording system at the Emergency Communications Center. A 2027 budget work session follows at 11:30.

On the roads, the State Route 73 resurfacing and turn lane project between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road keeps lane restrictions in place through November 30.
