Good morning, Waynesville. It's Wednesday, November 18, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect a sunny day with a high around 63 and a low near 35 tonight. Thursday looks sunny again with a high of 63, then Friday and Saturday warm to around 67, both sunny. There are no weather alerts in effect this morning.

Now the top stories.

Downtown Waynesville turns into Stars Hollow this weekend. Town Square Days runs from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. on both Saturday and Sunday, rain or shine, presented by the Waynesville Merchant Association. On Saturday at 11:30 a.m., Turn Out Dance Academy on South Main Street hosts a dance marathon. Entry is two canned goods or a five dollar donation for the Waynesville Food Pantry.

Ohio's youth deer gun season is this Saturday and Sunday. Caesar Creek State Park reminds visitors that hunting is allowed in several areas near its hiking, mountain bike and horse trails, and the park suggests wearing hunter orange if you head into the woods.

And the library's holiday season opens Friday. Bidding on the Friends of the Library Holiday Auction opens at noon Friday and runs until 7 p.m. on December 10. The Letters to Santa mailbox opens the same day, and letters dropped off by December 15 get a reply from Santa's elves.

In schools and sports, the Spartans play Bishop Fenwick in the Division Five, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. Friday at Lakota East High School. The whole village is invited to line the route for a send-off at 4:30 p.m. as the buses leave the high school, turn onto Main Street, and head out on State Route 73. The district is also looking for a new head varsity boys soccer coach.

A couple of things on the calendar. Tonight, Waynesville F C holds supplemental girls tryouts from 5:30 to 7 p.m. on the high school turf. On Thursday at 10:30 a.m., the Mary L. Cook Public Library hosts a service dog storytime. At 5 p.m., the Chamber's business to business networking meets at A Chick with Wicks, and the Waynesville Elementary P T O meets at 7 p.m.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet next Tuesday, November 24, at 9 a.m. in Lebanon. The Wayne Township Trustees meet December 1 at 7 p.m.

On the roads, expect lane restrictions on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through November 30 for resurfacing work.
