Good morning, Waynesville. It's Thursday, November 5, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect a mostly sunny day with a high of 66 and a low tonight of 44. Friday turns cooler, with a slight chance of light rain early and a high of 57. The weekend looks partly sunny to sunny, with highs in the mid to upper 50s and lows near freezing. There are no National Weather Service alerts this morning.

Now, three things to know.

The holiday shopping season starts downtown tomorrow. Hearthwarming Holidays runs Friday through Sunday, with shops open from 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. Friday and Saturday, and from noon to 5 p.m. Sunday. Nearby, the CrossView Christmas Bazaar at CrossView Christian Church on East Social Row Road is Friday from 3 to 7 p.m. and Saturday from 9 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Friday night, the Spartans varsity football team hosts Preble Shawnee at home, with kickoff at 7 p.m.

And on Tuesday, the Warren County Engineer comes to the village office on Lytle Road at 7 p.m. to talk about the North Warren County Transportation Plan and what's next for Waynesville's roads. The plan's online survey stays open through November 17.

In the schools, Wayne Local has posted its November and December events and activities newsletter, a monthly roundup of school and community happenings. The Waynesville Leo Club welcomed 50 new members and inducted six new officers Sunday night, the district said. The Board of Education meets Monday at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center, with Spartan Recognition Awards.

At the Mary L. Cook Public Library, Saturday's Gingerbread 101 Workshop runs from 11 a.m. to 1:30 p.m., and a Paint Party starts at 10 that morning. Preschool Storytime is this morning at 10:30, and the Teen Anime and Manga Club meets this afternoon at 3.

In local government, the county commissioners have not yet posted an agenda for Tuesday's session. Wayne Township is also pointing residents to the transportation plan ahead of Tuesday's presentation.

On the roads, the State Route 73 resurfacing and turn lane project between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road continues, with lane restrictions and flaggers through November 30.

Looking ahead, the Waynesville Fantasy Festival comes downtown Saturday, November 14, and Owl Fest is at Caesar Creek State Park that same afternoon and evening. In Lebanon, The City Mercantile Christmas Market fills the Warren County Fairgrounds November 13 through 15.
