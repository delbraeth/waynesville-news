Good morning, Waynesville. It's Saturday, November 21, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. It's a sunny start, with a high near 68 and a low around 43 tonight. Sunday looks much the same, sunny with a high near 68. A chance of light rain comes in Monday before the sun returns, and it turns cooler, with a high of 52 Monday and 45 on Tuesday. There are no weather alerts this morning.

Now, the big news. The Spartans are regional champions. Waynesville shut out Bishop Fenwick 27 to nothing Friday night at Lakota East to win the Division Five, Region 20 title. The athletic department says it is the first trip to the state semifinals in school history. State Final Four shirts can be ordered through 9 p.m. Sunday, with pickup on Wednesday from 3 to 5 p.m. Watch the Spartans Athletics page for the semifinal opponent and site.

Second, school finances. Wayne Local's November newsletter says this year's forecast projects about 20.4 million dollars in revenue and 22.1 million in expenses, which means drawing about 1.7 million dollars from reserves. The district says those reserves are projected to be nearly used up by the end of fiscal year 2030. Wayne Local will hold a Community Levy Listening Session on Monday, December 7, at 6 p.m. in the Performing Arts Center lobby. The district wants input on whether a levy should go on the ballot in May or November of 2027, and whether it should be a property tax or an earned income tax. The school board takes up the question again at its December 14 meeting.

Third, the Stars Hollow weekend continues downtown. Town Square Days runs from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. today and 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Sunday. The dance marathon at Turn Out Dance Academy on South Main Street starts at 11:30 this morning.

In schools, there's no school all next week for Thanksgiving break. When students return, the Spartan canned food drive runs from November 30 through December 11. Waynesville Elementary will hold its first-ever Spartans Got Talent show on December 10, and the middle and high school art exhibition is that same evening, from 4 to 7 p.m. at the Performing Arts Center. Families of bus riders are reminded to keep notifications on in the My Ride K12 app, since urgent transportation notices go out only there.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet Tuesday at 9 a.m. in Lebanon, and the Wayne Township trustees meet December 1 at 7 p.m.

On the roads, resurfacing on State Route 73 between Red Lion-Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road keeps lane restrictions in place through November 30. And it's youth deer gun season this weekend, so Caesar Creek State Park reminds visitors that hunting is allowed in some areas near the trails.

That's your brief. Read the full edition, with links to every source, at waynesville dot news.
