Good morning, Waynesville. It's Sunday, November 15, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect a mostly cloudy day with a high of 50 and a low tonight of 31. The week ahead looks dry and warmer: mostly sunny Monday with a high of 54, then sunny Tuesday at 58 and Wednesday at 62. The National Weather Service has no alerts in effect.

Our top story this morning: it's your last chance to see Radium Girls. Waynesville's high school theater closes its run of the play at 2 p.m. today at the Performing Arts Center on Dayton Road. Tickets are 10 dollars for students and 12 dollars for adults, sold at the door, and you can pay with cash or card.

Next, the Spartans are still waiting to learn where they'll play. After Friday night's 14 to 7 win at Graham, Waynesville meets Bishop Fenwick in the Division Five, Region 20 final at 7 p.m. this Friday. Regional finals are played at neutral sites, and as of this morning the state athletic association had not announced this one.

And it's a full week for local government. Village Council meets Monday. On Tuesday morning, the Warren County commissioners will consider a quote for geotechnical exploration work for the Waynesville wastewater treatment plant improvements, along with a public hearing on changes to the county's subdivision regulations. The Wayne Township trustees meet Tuesday at 7 p.m. at the township building on North Clarksville Road.

In school news, wrestling families have two meetings this week in the middle and high school cafeteria: seventh and eighth grade at 4:20 Monday afternoon, and high school at 6 p.m. Tuesday. The board of education next meets December 14.

Around town, the Chamber's Business to Business mixer is Thursday at 5 p.m. at A Chick with Wicks. And next weekend, downtown Waynesville steps into Stars Hollow for Town Square Days, 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Saturday and Sunday, rain or shine. If you're headed to Lebanon today, The City Mercantile Christmas Market wraps up at the Warren County Fairgrounds, open until 3 p.m. Out at Caesar Creek State Park, naturalists host a cast iron cooking program at the Nature Center next Saturday from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m.

On the roads, expect lane restrictions on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through the end of the month for resurfacing and a new turn lane.
