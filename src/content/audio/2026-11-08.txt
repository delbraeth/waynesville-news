Good morning, Waynesville. It's Sunday, November 8, and this is the Daily Brief from Waynesville News.

First, the weather. Expect widespread frost early, then a sunny day with a high near 58 and a low tonight around 35. Monday looks much the same, with frost giving way to sunshine and a high near 60. Tuesday turns milder and sunny, with a high near 65. There are no weather alerts in effect.

Now, the top stories.

It was a big weekend for Spartan athletics. The Waynesville boys cross country team finished seventh as a team at Saturday's state meet, and the team's top runner placed twenty-eighth overall in sixteen minutes, eight point eight seconds, according to Wayne Local Schools. And the football team, fresh off Friday's 35 to 13 playoff win over Preble Shawnee, travels to Graham Local in St. Paris this Friday at 7 p.m. for a regional semifinal.

Today is the last day of Hearthwarming Holidays downtown. Shops are open from noon to 5 p.m. for the final day of the Merchants Association's holiday shopping weekend. Also today, American Legion Post 615 holds its annual Veterans Day dinner, starting at 3 p.m.

And on Tuesday at 7 p.m., Warren County Engineer Kurt Weber will discuss the North Warren County Transportation Plan at the Village of Waynesville office on Lytle Road. The Chamber asks attendees to let them know they're coming. The plan's online survey stays open through November 17.

In schools, the Wayne Local Board of Education holds its regular monthly meeting Monday at 6 p.m. in the auditorium of the Performing Arts Center on Dayton Road, with Spartan Recognition Awards on the program. No delays or closings are posted.

In local government, the Warren County Commissioners meet Tuesday morning. Up for consideration are contracts for an apron and taxiway project at the county airport, spending opioid settlement funds for the county drug task force, and starting negotiations to replace the recording system at the county's Emergency Communications Center. A 2027 budget work session follows at 11:30.

Looking ahead to next Saturday, the Waynesville Fantasy Festival comes downtown, and Owl Fest runs from 3 to 11 p.m. at Caesar Creek State Park.

On the roads, lane restrictions continue on State Route 73 between Red Lion Five Points Road and Harveysburg Road through the end of November.
