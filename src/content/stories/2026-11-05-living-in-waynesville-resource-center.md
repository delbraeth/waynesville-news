Where do you pay the water bill? What day does trash go out? Who do you call about a utility problem after hours? A new page on Waynesville News, [Living in Waynesville](/resources/), puts those answers in one place.

The page collects the phone numbers, addresses and links residents look for most. Every entry comes from the office's or organization's own website, links back to that source and shows the date we last checked it. Phone numbers can be tapped to call on a phone.

## What's on it

- **Emergencies and public safety:** 911; Warren County's non-emergency dispatch line, 513-695-2525, which the village also lists for utility emergencies after hours; the Waynesville Police Department; the Wayne Township Fire Department; the Warren County Sheriff's Office; and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
- **Village of Waynesville:**
  - **Office:** 1400 Lytle Road, 513-897-8015. Open weekdays 8 a.m. to noon and 1 to 4 p.m.
  - **Bills:** every way to pay the water, sewer and trash bill.
  - **Trash:** Rumpke picks up trash, recycling and yard waste on Mondays, so set it out Sunday evening.
  - **Also:** fall leaf pickup, streets and snow, the drinking water quality report, council meetings and zoning.
- **Wayne Township:** Township Hall at 6050 N. Clarksville Road (513-897-3010), plus the trustees, zoning and roads.
- **Warren County:** the commissioners, Board of Elections, auditor's real estate office and county engineer.
- **Community help:**
  - **Food pantry:** the Waynesville Food Pantry and Wayne Township Community Aid at St. Mary's Episcopal Church, 107 S. Third St. It's open Monday, Wednesday and Friday, 3 to 4:30 p.m., and also helps with utility bills.
  - **Seniors:** Warren County Community Services' Elderly Services Program for residents 60 and older.
- **Schools and library:** the Wayne Local Schools central office and the Mary L. Cook Public Library.

## Also new: submit an event

Community groups, churches, schools and clubs can now send events for the [events calendar](/events/) using a short [submission form](/events/submit/). It asks for the name, date and time, place, organizer and cost, then opens a pre-filled email to the editor. Every submission is reviewed before it's listed, and events open to the public are listed free.

## Help keep it current

Offices change hours and phone numbers. If you spot something out of date, or know a resource that belongs on the page, [email the editor](mailto:editor@waynesville.news?subject=Resource%20correction).
