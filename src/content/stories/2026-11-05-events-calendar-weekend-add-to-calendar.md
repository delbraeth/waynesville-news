The [Waynesville News events calendar](/events/) has three new tools to help you plan your week.

**This weekend.** Switch from "All upcoming" to "This weekend" to see only what's happening Friday through Sunday. Early in the week it shows the coming weekend; from Friday on, it shows the rest of the current one. You can share the weekend view directly: [waynesville.news/events/?when=weekend](/events/?when=weekend).

**Waynesville area only.** The calendar includes some bigger regional draws, like WinterFest at Kings Island and the Christmas market at the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon. Those are now marked "Regional." Check "Waynesville area only" to hide them, along with the county commissioners' weekly sessions in Lebanon. The page remembers your choice.

**Add to calendar.** Every upcoming event now has "Add to calendar" links. **Apple / Outlook** downloads a calendar file for iPhone, Mac or Outlook, and **Google** opens Google Calendar with the event filled in. Each entry includes the place, the details link and a link back to our calendar. Events that run several days, like a weekend festival, are added as all-day events with the daily hours in the notes.

## On the calendar this weekend

- **Friday, 7 p.m.: Spartans playoff football, Round 2.** After a first-round bye, Waynesville hosts Preble Shawnee in the second round of the OHSAA playoffs at Spartan Community Field.
  - **Tickets:** The Ohio High School Athletic Association sets the prices: $12 for adults and $5 for students.
  - **Paying:** Cash is taken at the gate. To pay by card, buy online through OHSAA; student tickets go on sale online after 4 p.m. Friday.
  - **Passes:** Waynesville athletic passes and senior passes don't work for playoff games.
  - Details are on the [school's ticket flyer](/images/events/2026-11-06-football-playoff-preble-shawnee.jpg).
- **Friday through Sunday: Hearthwarming Holidays** in the downtown shops.
- **Friday and Saturday: CrossView Christmas Bazaar** at CrossView Christian Church.
- **Saturday: Gingerbread 101 Workshop** at the Mary L. Cook Public Library.

## Have an event to add?

Churches, schools, clubs and community groups can send events for the calendar with our [event submission form](/events/submit/). Every submission is reviewed before it's listed, and public events in and around Waynesville are listed free.
