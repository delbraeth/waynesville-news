Sun Sprouts Cafe is at **69 S. Maple St. in Corwin**, on the Little Miami Bike Path. It's open **Wednesday through Sunday, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m.**, and closed Mondays and Tuesdays. Phone: [513-855-1075](tel:+15138551075). "We focus on offering high quality, made from scratch food," the menu says.

This is the cafe's printed winter menu as of Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026, typed out from the paper copy. Prices and items can change, so check with the cafe. The printed menu also marks dairy-free, vegan and gluten-free items; ask staff when you order.

## Smoothies
All smoothies are made with maple and whole milk.

- **Tropical Dream** — pineapple, strawberries, mango and peaches. $8.00
- **Mixed Berry** — strawberries, blueberries, blackberries and raspberries. $8.00
- **Strawberry Banana** — strawberries and bananas. $8.00
- **Ginger Immunity** — pineapple, mango, banana, pomegranate, passion fruit, camu camu and ginger. $10.50
- **Coco Monkey** — peanut butter, chocolate chips, cocoa powder and bananas. $8.00
- **Cherry Garcia** — cherries, strawberries, cocoa powder, chocolate chips and almond extract. $8.00
- **Antioxidant Beet** — blueberries, red seedless grapes, cherries, beets and turmeric. $10.50

Substitute almond milk, coconut milk, oat milk or orange juice for 50¢.

**Add-ins:** 10g protein powder $2 · iced coffee $2 · Greek yogurt $1 · turmeric 50¢ · peanut butter $1 · almond butter $1.50 · spinach 75¢ · ginger shot $1

## Breakfast
- **Caprese Avocado Toast** — avocado, tomato, mozzarella, basil and balsamic glaze. $11.00 (add an egg, $2)

## Sandwiches
All sandwiches come with your choice of dressed greens, potato chips or pickle slices. Choice of bread: sourdough, wheat, gluten-free (+50¢) or a wrap.

- **Beet Reuben** — cooked beets with "pastrami seasoning," Thousand Island, sauerkraut, Swiss and dark rye. $13.00
- **Gouda & Apple Panini** — smoked Gouda with apple and the cafe's maple mustard. $10.00
- **Deviled Egg Salad** — eggs with mayo, mustard, green onions and pickles, toasted with spring greens. $10.00
- **Albacore Tuna Salad** — tuna with mayo, mustard, red onions, celery and pickles, toasted with spring greens. $12.00

## Allergens and payment
Per the menu, these major food allergens are used as ingredients at the cafe: milk, egg, fish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy and sesame. Let staff know if you have questions about them.

The menu also notes that a "cost of acceptance" fee is applied toward credit card fees.
