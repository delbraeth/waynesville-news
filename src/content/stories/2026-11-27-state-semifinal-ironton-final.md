Ironton beat Waynesville **35-10** Friday night in the **Division V state semifinal** at Teays Valley High School in Ashville, ending the Spartans' season at **11-3**.

It was the first state semifinal in Waynesville history. The Spartans earned the trip by winning the Region 20 championship, beating Bishop Fenwick 27-0 on Nov. 20 at Lakota East, a week after a 14-7 win at Graham. The day began with the All-Village Spartan Send-Off, as the team's buses left Waynesville High School at 3:45 p.m.

Ironton, the Region 19 champion, advances to the Division V state championship game on Friday, Dec. 4.
