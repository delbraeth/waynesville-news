Bentino's Pizza is at **140 S. Main St., Waynesville**. Phone: [513-897-1600](tel:+15138971600). Its website lists hours of 11 a.m. to 10 p.m. Sunday through Thursday and 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. Friday and Saturday.

This is the menu posted on [Bentino's website](http://www.bentinos-pizza.com/menu), typed out as text so it's easier to read on a phone. It's current as of Saturday, Nov. 28, 2026. Prices and items can change, so check with Bentino's before you go.

## Create your own pizza
Start with Bentino's marinara or olive oil and garlic sauce, cheese and your favorite toppings.

- **Small (8-inch)** — $8.95, plus $2.25 per topping
- **Medium (12-inch)** — $12.25, plus $2.99 per topping
- **X-large (16-inch)** — $14.99, plus $3.75 per topping

**Toppings:** ham, sausage, pepperoni, real bacon, green peppers, banana peppers, jalapeño peppers, mushrooms, black olives, green olives, onions, tomatoes, pineapple, extra cheese

## Specialty pizzas
Small (8-inch) $13.95 · medium (12-inch) $22.95 · x-large (16-inch) $28.99

- **Meat Madness** — pepperoni, sausage, ham and bacon with cheese
- **Deluxe** — pepperoni, sausage, mushrooms, onions, green peppers and cheese
- **Hawaiian Paradise** — pineapple, ham, Bentino's marinara and cheese, sprinkled with cinnamon
- **Very Veggie** — mushrooms, onions, green peppers, tomatoes and black olives with cheese
- **The Four Cheese** — olive oil and garlic sauce with bacon, feta, mozzarella, provolone and Parmesan
- **The Greek** — olive oil and garlic sauce, fresh spinach, Roma tomatoes and sliced onions with mozzarella and feta
- **Gus's White Pizza** — homemade Alfredo sauce with spinach, mushrooms and onions, topped with provolone, mozzarella and Parmesan

## Calzones
Made with homemade dough and a mozzarella and provolone blend, stuffed with your choice of 3 ingredients and served with Bentino's marinara for dipping.

- **Regular** — $10.99 (extra toppings $2.25 each)
- **Jumbo** — $16.99 (extra toppings $2.99 each)

## Salads
Add chicken to any salad for $3.

- **House Salad** — iceberg lettuce with cheese and tomatoes. $4.99
- **Chef Salad** — iceberg lettuce with ham, pepperoni, cheese and tomatoes. $11.99
- **Greek Salad** — iceberg lettuce with black olives, banana peppers, tomatoes, feta and homemade Greek dressing. $11.99
- **Garden Salad** — spinach and lettuce with green peppers, tomatoes, onions, and black and green olives. $11.99
- **Grilled Chicken Salad** — spinach and lettuce with grilled chicken, tomatoes, bacon and onions. $14.99

**Dressings:** golden Italian, honey French, Thousand Island, buttermilk ranch, lite ranch, homemade Greek

## Pasta
Served with garlic bread. Add a house salad for $3.

- **Cheese Ravioli** — jumbo cheese ravioli with Bentino's marinara. $12.99
- **Chicken Alfredo** — spaghetti with chicken breast in homemade Alfredo sauce. $13.99
- **Spaghetti and Meatballs** — in Bentino's marinara. $12.99 (extra meatballs $1.75)

## Hoagies and gyros
All sandwiches are $9.99 and come with chips.

- **Steak** — homemade marinara or mushroom sauce with mozzarella and onions
- **Italian** — ham, salami and pepperoni with lettuce, tomatoes, Italian dressing and cheese
- **Supreme** — ham and salami with Bentino's marinara, onions and cheese
- **Meatball** — Italian meatballs, homemade marinara, onions and mozzarella
- **Cajun Chicken** — chicken breast with Cajun spices, lettuce, cheese and ranch
- **Chicken Parmesan** — boneless chicken breast with Bentino's marinara and cheese
- **Hawaiian Chicken** — boneless chicken breast with pineapple, lettuce and mayo
- **Bentino's Gyro** — toasted pita with gyro meat, onions, diced tomatoes and house-made tzatziki

Add extra cheese to any hoagie for $1.75, or a side of any Bentino's sauce for $1.25.
