The first big light shows of the season open next week, and our [Holiday Lights guide](/holiday-lights/) now lists 12 displays within an easy drive of Waynesville. Each one has dates, hours and prices taken from the organizer's own website. We check those pages every Monday through the season. Hours and prices can still change, so check the organizer's site before you go.

## Close to home

**Yuletide Village: Season of Lights** returns Nov. 27 at Renaissance Park, 10542 E. State Route 73, on the Renaissance Festival grounds. It's a walk-through holiday village with a "Rivers of Lights" area, a market, carolers, firepits, and visits from Santa and Krampus. It's open Fridays 5–9 p.m. and Saturdays 3–9 p.m. through Dec. 19, with tickets sold online.

Downtown has its own Christmas weekends:

- **Christmas in the Village, Dec. 4–6.** Santa arrives by fire truck at the gazebo on Main Street Friday at 6 p.m. and Saturday at 1 p.m. Sunday brings the Christmas Market Street Faire and the Parade of Santas at 1 p.m.
- **Waynesville Christmas Rewind, Saturday, Dec. 12, 4–9 p.m.** Last-minute local shopping, hot cocoa and classic-Christmas fun downtown. It's free.

The [full events calendar](/events/) has more, including the Lions Club pancake breakfast with Santa on Dec. 5 and the library's Santa open house that same morning.

## In Warren County

- **The Christmas Ranch**, 3205 S. Waynesville Road, just south of the village near Morrow, opens Nov. 20. It has light displays, heated shops, a nativity, model trains, and train or wagon rides to Santa's House. A carload costs $27–37 online, depending on the date, or $39 at the gate.
- **Cincinnati's Nights of Lights** at the Warren County Fairgrounds in Lebanon also opens Nov. 20 and runs nightly through Jan. 2. It's a nearly 2-mile drive through more than 2 million lights. Tickets are timed: you choose a 30-minute arrival window when you buy. A carload of up to 8 costs $30 plus a $3 fee, and tickets are non-refundable.
- **WinterFest at Kings Island** in Mason runs select nights from Nov. 27 through Dec. 31. Guests 65 and older can get in free with the park's 2026 Senior Admission. You register online, with a limit of two per order, and bring ID to the gate. Parking isn't included.
- **Woodland Lights** at Countryside Park in Washington Township is a free walk-through display, open select nights from Nov. 20, 6–9 p.m.

## Worth the drive

- **Cincinnati Zoo's PNC Festival of Lights** opens first, on Nov. 19. It's included with zoo admission.
- **Clifton Mill's Legendary Light Display** opens Nov. 27. It costs $15 a person, age 4 and up, paid at the gate.
- **A Carillon Christmas** in Dayton opens Nov. 24.
- **Pyramid Hill Lights** in Hamilton runs from Nov. 20 to Jan. 3.
- **Hillsboro's Winter Wonderland**, new to our guide this week, is a 5,000-foot drive-through trail at the Highland County Fairgrounds. It runs select weekends from Nov. 27 into January, at $25 per vehicle.

Two Middletown favorites, Christmas Glow at Land of Illusion and Light Up Middletown, haven't posted this year's dates yet. We'll add them to the guide when they do.
