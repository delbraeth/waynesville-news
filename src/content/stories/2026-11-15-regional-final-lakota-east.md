Waynesville's Division V, Region 20 final against Bishop Fenwick will be played at **Lakota East High School**, 6840 Lakota Lane in Liberty Township, the Spartans announced Sunday. Kickoff is **7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 20**.

Regional finals are played at neutral sites. Waynesville reached the final with a 14-7 win at Graham on Friday. Bishop Fenwick, from Franklin, won 27-13 at Williamsburg.

Check the Spartans' pages for ticket details. The game is on our [events calendar](/events/), and the [game page on MaxPreps](https://www.maxpreps.com/oh/football/game/bishop-fenwick-franklin-vs-waynesville/11-20-2026/?c=2f2a6d51-5706-4af5-a1bd-43c71e2f70d3) will carry the score.
