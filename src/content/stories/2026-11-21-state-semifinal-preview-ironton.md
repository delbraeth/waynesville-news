Waynesville is in the state semifinals for the first time in school history. The Spartans (11-2) meet Ironton (12-1) in a **Division V state semifinal at 7 p.m. Friday, Nov. 27, at Teays Valley High School in Ashville**, south of Columbus. Check the Spartans' and OHSAA pages for ticket details.

**How Waynesville got here.** The Spartans won the Region 20 title Friday night, shutting out Bishop Fenwick 27-0 at Lakota East. Their playoff run started with a 35-13 home win over Preble Shawnee and a 14-7 win at Graham. Their two losses came in league play, at Oakwood and Carlisle; they have won six straight since.

**The opponent.** Ironton, from the Ohio River city in Lawrence County, won the Region 19 final 49-0 over Fort Frye. The Fighting Tigers have outscored their three playoff opponents 133-0, and their only loss this season was 47-40 in overtime to Big Walnut on Sept. 25. The Ironton Tribune reports that Ironton is after its 13th state finals appearance and its fourth state championship.

**What's next.** The winner plays the Region 17–Region 18 champion for the Division V state title at 10:30 a.m. Friday, Dec. 4, and the OHSAA says the winner of this semifinal will be the home team.
