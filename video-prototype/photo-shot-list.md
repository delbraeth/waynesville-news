# Photo list for the Daily Brief video

Priority comes from how often each topic shows up across the 103 published
briefs (soccer, volleyball, football, the commissioners, roads and festivals
lead). Get the **Tier 1** set first; it covers most days.

**Stock** = a generic Envato Elements image is fine. It shows a "Stock image"
label on screen.
**Local** = viewers will expect the real place, so use our own photo. Stock
would mislead here. Leave the slide on a map or text until we have one.

## What to look for in every image
- Landscape, at least 2400 px wide. The main subject should sit near the
  middle: the video crops to a 6:5 card beside the text, and sometimes uses the
  full 16:9 frame.
- No readable text, brand logos, license plates or house numbers. No other
  school's name, logo or mascot.
- No recognizable faces of children. Adults only from behind, at a distance or
  out of focus. Skip anything marked "editorial use only".
- Calm, natural colour, not oversaturated. Daylight or golden hour reads best
  next to the cream page.
- File name: `<topic>-<n>.jpg` (e.g. `soccer-1.jpg`). Note **stock** or
  **ours** and the source/credit for each in a short list or a reply.

## Skip these on purpose
- **Obituaries:** never illustrate them.
- **Crashes, crime, sheriff or prosecutor news:** no stock "police lights" or
  "crime scene" images. They imply something we haven't reported. Use text or
  a map.
- **Specific businesses or churches:** only their own photo, given with
  permission, never a look-alike.
